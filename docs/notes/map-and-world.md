# Map 1 (block city), Map Editor, open world, Play

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-16 (word for word) so it is only read when needed. Newest notes for a topic go at the end of its file.

- **Map-making rules (standing, 2026-10-01 - Gaymi's feedback on Map 3).** Any generated or hand-built map (`Map3Generator.js` and any future one): (1) use only block types that already exist in `BLOCK_TYPES`; (2) not "squared" - never one identical box per lot lined up in rows: split blocks into several buildings of different widths, depths, heights and setbacks, mix shapes (L, U, notched, setback towers, pitched roofs), leave alleys/yards between them, and scatter trees/lamps/props off-grid; (3) close the map in with a natural-looking ring of buildings along the edge (uneven heights, different materials), plus a ragged rock wall on every border cell the ring leaves open (tallest at the edge, lower inward) - no spot may show empty sky; **exception since 2026-10-12: Map 1's road ends are open gates into the open world**; since 2026-10-16 Map 1 isn't a square at all (`CityShape.js`, see the Open World note) - its ring of edge buildings follows the uneven outline and there are no rock walls or barricades; (4) never leave a hole under water/lava - every liquid cell gets a solid bottom and solid sides so nothing falls through; (5) ladders always have a wall or support right behind them and stay indoors - on a tower that steps in higher up, put the ladder inside the TOP tier (with a support column below the step), and drop any ladder left with nothing beside it after ruin holes are carved (reported 2026-10-01: ladders "hanging on nowhere"); (6) sloped roofs only on plain, whole rectangles, rising half a block per cell (slab, full block, slab...) - full-block steps on L-shapes read as a messy staircase; (7) **no ruined buildings at all** - ragged tops, blown-out holes and smashed windows all read as "broken" to Gaymi (reported twice); keep every building whole; (8) **no street grid** - from the top-down view an even grid of equal square blocks reads as "16x16"; use winding/bent/diagonal roads, uneven spacing, roads that end in T-junctions and a roundabout, so blocks come out in different sizes and shapes (Map 3 does this with road polylines + a distance field, then flood-fills the land into blocks and cuts every building's footprint to its block - `building(box, style, facade, mask)`); (9) **every building can be entered and climbed to the top** (reported 2026-10-02: tall buildings had no way up): each gets a door on a straight stretch of wall whose inside is a real room and whose outside is open (`SIDES` search in `building()`, facade side first), a half-block step outside it (the ground floor is a block above the street), and a ladder through every floor and, on flat roofs, through a roof hatch - or (2026-10-03, "not all ladders") a real staircase instead in about half the buildings (`findStairwell`/`buildStairs` in `Map3Generator.js`: a 6x2 strip along an inside wall, flights going back and forth between its two lanes, stairs of the building's floor material facing the climb, three cells of headroom over every stair - two wasn't enough to step up - and the 2-high ground floor's ceiling opened in front of the first stair; chosen by its own seed, and the furniture/roof-clutter picks that skip the stairwell still draw their random number, so the rest of the city is unchanged); nothing may stand in the doorway or in front of the ladder (props are kept clear, a building clears its lot first so earlier trees don't end up inside, trees never grow through anything, low leaves in front of a door are trimmed, a door left facing a later neighbor's wall gets a second door through it, edge-ring buildings never overlap at corners, setback towers only where the top tier still has a room); (10) check automatically that every ladder has a wall beside it, every water cell has a solid bottom and sides, and every building's top is reachable from the street (a walking flood-fill over the generated blocks - 73/73 on the current seed), then verify by flying the editor camera around it (overview + street level + close-ups of houses/towers) and looking, not just by counting blocks.

- **Map 1 performance (2026-10-06, "20-50fps on my old phone").** Measured first: from a street the city drew up to ~300 draws / 250k triangles, a full zombie wave added ~130 draws (8 meshes each), and the real killer was the zombie flow field - one `_rebuildFlow` pass took ~65ms on a fast PC (several hundred ms on a phone) and ran every 0.5s plus on every cell the player stepped into, i.e. a freeze twice a second. Fixes: the flow field now runs as a time-sliced job (`_startFlow`/`_stepFlow`, `FLOW_BUDGET_MS` per frame, zombies follow the last finished field; numeric `cellKey` keys and a per-pass `_memo` of cell solidity); zombies are drawn as one `InstancedMesh` per body part (`_zombieBatch`/`drawZombies`, called from `BuildMode.render()`; each zombie keeps a cloned body only for its part matrices, hit flash is `instanceColor`, `_removeZombie` must not dispose the shared geometry); **View Distance** (Settings > Graphics, `settings.viewDistance`, `VIEW_DISTANCES` in BuildMode.js) hides chunks past it (`BlockChunks.cullTo`, nearest-edge distance, never the camera far plane - the night stars sit 150 units out) with `scene.fog` (always present so it never recompiles shaders) colored from the sky every frame; Auto starts at Max on a computer and Medium on touch devices (`TOUCH_DEVICE`) and Auto Quality steps it down once resolution is already at its floor (`stepAutoView`, given back first when there's headroom); camp NPCs past it are hidden; leaves are a `cutout` chunk kind (alpha-tested, depth-writing) instead of blended; touch devices get anisotropy 1. Measure with the renderer's draw/triangle counts and a CDP `Emulation.setCPUThrottlingRate` profile - headless frame times are software-rendered and mean little on their own.

- **Phone controls (2026-10-06, "make it simple, icons not words") - `BuildTouch.js` (`buildMode.touch`).** Only on touch devices (`isTouchDevice`: hover none + coarse pointer) and only while walking the map (`tryMode.active` - Map 1 Play and Try Map; building stays keyboard/mouse). One full-screen `#touch-play` overlay: a floating stick in the left 45% (`_touchMove` {x, y, run}, read by `BuildTryMode.update` as analog movement - part-way pushes walk slower, past `RUN_AT` runs, up/down also climbs ladders), drag anywhere else to look (Mouse Sensitivity/Invert Look apply), and SVG icon buttons with no text: Fire (hold repeats every `FIRE_REPEAT_S`), Jump (holds `Space` in `_keys`), Reload, Use (`_tryUseFromCamera` - doors, chests, NPCs), Crouch (toggles `KeyC`), Pause (`toggleMenu`). Everything goes through the keyboard/mouse paths, so behavior can't differ. The buttons hide under the pause menu, an NPC panel or game over; `body.touch-play-on` (whole time you're in the map) hides the hotbar/chat/tool hint, shrinks the minimap and moves ammo, wave line and the fps readout out of the thumbs' way. Test with `isMobile`/`hasTouch` and CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent` (tests/buildmode.spec.js "on a phone") - `touchStart` with several points re-sends earlier fingers as new, so test one finger at a time.

- **Auto Quality (Settings > Graphics, on by default) now only drives the Map Editor/Map 1's resolution** (`_updateEditorResScale`, reads `settings.autoQuality`); the old Map 1's `AutoQuality.js` controller (view distance, zombie cap, light count) was deleted with it on 2026-10-06. Don't add a second automatic system that writes the editor's resolution.

- **Map editor blocks are chunk meshes, not one InstancedMesh per type (since 2026-09-29).** `BlockChunks.js` cuts the world into 16x16x16 chunks and meshes only faces touching air/see-through blocks, with Minecraft-style per-vertex corner shading, all block textures in one atlas (so one draw call per chunk and material kind). Measured in the software renderer: ~1450ms -> ~60ms per frame, and ~200k -> ~32k triangles for the default floor. `BuildMode.placeBlock`/`removeBlock` only update `_blocks`/`_chunkCells` and mark chunks dirty; `render()` rebuilds dirty chunks once per frame. Only shaped blocks (stairs/fence/ladder) still use `_instancedMeshes`. Ladders hang flat against the first solid neighbor (`_ladderMatrix`, `LADDER_SIDES`; re-checked after a bulk load and whenever a neighbor is placed/broken) instead of standing in the middle of their cell (2026-10-02 report: "looks weird"). The editor has **no point lights at all** (since 2026-09-30): glowing blocks light their surroundings through baked vertex light in the chunk meshes (`BlockChunks.setGlow`/`_glowAt`, Minecraft-style block light, rebuilt with the chunks in its radius) - the 6 light proxies it had before cost ~35% of every editor frame even with nothing glowing nearby. Don't add real lights to the editor scene. Shaped blocks use MeshLambertMaterial like the chunks. The editor auto-lowers resolution to hold 60 fps (or the FPS Cap) (`_updateEditorResScale` in Game.js, Auto Quality setting): Retina extra pixels go first, and only a device still slow at 1 pixel per CSS pixel goes lower, floored at `EDITOR_MIN_PIXEL_RATIO` (0.6). With the Shadows setting on, the editor's shadow map is redrawn only when chunks rebuild (`_shadowsDirty` in `BuildMode.render()`, `shadowMap.autoUpdate` off just for that render) - the sun never moves. MSAA is off on high-DPI touch devices (`highDpiTouch` in Game.js). The editor renders without the survival game's ACES tone mapping. Two old bugs fixed on the way: `_rgb()` wrote THREE.Color's *linear* components into canvas fillStyles, drawing every block texture ~3x too dark (use `getHex()`/`getStyle()` for canvas colors - `new THREE.Color(hex).r` is linear, not the hex's sRGB value), and `getImageData` on a GPU-backed canvas made the editor take ~7s to open (texture canvases use `willReadFrequently: true`). Block textures are Minecraft-style 16x16 pixel art painted procedurally by `BlockTextures.js` (seeded per block id, per-face top/side/bottom for grass/logs/bookshelf/TNT/pumpkin/hay/cactus/sandstone/bone); `blockIconURL()` draws the isometric inventory icons the picker and hotbar show. A new block type falls back to its `pattern` name there - give it a proper painter in `BY_ID`/`OVERRIDE` rather than relying on the fallback. **Water and lava flow like Minecraft (`LiquidFlow.js`, `buildMode.liquids`, 2026-10-03):** a placed block is a source; every water tick (0.25s) / lava tick (1.5s) liquid falls first, then spreads sideways (water 7, lava 3 via step 2) toward the nearest drop within 4/2 cells, dries up when nothing feeds it, two water sources make a third, and lava touching water becomes obsidian (source) / cobblestone (flowing). Flowing cells are ordinary `water`/`lava` entries in `_blocks` with their level in `liquids.levels` (`FALLING` = 99 - it once was 8, which a level-7 neighbor + 1 also produced, so drained water turned into undying "falling" cells); `_snapshot()` skips them (sources re-flow on load), the raycast passes through them, and the `placeBlock`/`removeBlock` wrappers (real work in `_placeBlockInner`/`_removeBlockInner`) replace a flow cell you build into and call `liquids.changed()`. The mesher slopes the surface by averaging corner heights (`_liquidCorners`, `liquidHeight` option). Water got bloxd.io's lighter, clearer blue, and in Try Map a head under water/lava tints the screen (`#build-try-underwater`). Try Map also drops the camera's near plane to 0.012 (`NEAR`, restored on exit) - at the flying camera's 0.1 (a third of a block) standing against a wall showed straight through it. Water/lava (`LIQUIDS` in `BlockChunks.js`) have their own scrolling textures (`animate()`, called from `BuildMode.render()`), a surface 2px below the cell top, never hide neighbors' faces, and don't block movement (`_blockedAt` skips them). Slabs are `shape: 'slab'` (half-height `CUSTOM_BLOCK_GEOMETRY.slab`), textured from the full block named in `SHAPED_TEXTURE_FROM`. Every full block also has a generated slab (`${id}slab`, `slabOf` = source id; generated right after `BLOCK_TYPES`), textured top/side/bottom from its source. Shaped blocks' InstancedMeshes are created lazily on first placement and grown by doubling (`_shapedMesh`) - never pre-allocate one per shaped type (with ~190 of them that was ~270 MB). Doors (2026-09-30, `shape: 'door'`) are two cells tall: the bottom is the picker type, the top is a hidden generated `${id}top` type (`hidden: true`, left out of `PICKER_TYPES`) placed/removed only together with it (`placeBlock`/`removeBlock`), each half showing its half of the 16x32 `doorCanvas`. Facing/open state lives in `_doorState` (both halves' keys), goes into the instance matrix (`_doorMatrix`), is saved as `facing`/`open` on the bottom entry (top halves aren't saved), and rides along in undo entries (`state`). Right-click on a door toggles it (`toggleDoor`) instead of placing; open doors don't block movement. TNT was replaced by C4 the same day - `LEGACY_TYPE_IDS` maps old saves' `tnt`/`tntslab` to `c4`/`c4slab`; add any future renamed/removed block id there rather than letting old builds silently drop it. **Saved builds are trusted as-is (fixed 2026-10-01):** `_applyBuild` only fills the grass ground layer for a slot with no ground in its data (never saved, or old/shared data without one) - it used to run `_ensureGroundLayer()` on every load, which put grass back over every cell the player had broken. - **Pyramids:** `pyramid()` in WorldStructures, desert squares with roll < 0.3 (before villages). Hollow, 2-thick stepped shell, passage on a random side.
- **Underground cities:** `UnderCity.js` pure `cityCell(c, x, y, z)` -> undefined / null (air) / block; one per `UC_GRID` square (chance `UC_CHANCE`), floor `FLOOR_MIN..FLOOR_MAX`. Underground treats them like caves (`_grow` marks air open and city blocks solid with their own type; `implicitSolid` and `reveal` ask the city first). Near a city, Underground grows `CITY_RADIUS`/`CITY_ABOVE` chunks with `CITY_BUDGET_MS`.
- **Top bar:** `TopBar.setFps(fps, detail, ms, gpu)`; `chipName()` shortens the WebGL renderer string; the Show FPS setting hides FPS, MS and chip together.
