# Themes (Old, Simple/Clean) and panel look rules

Split out of the old `ui-and-menu.md` on 2026-11-13 (word for word, oldest first). Add new notes at the end.

- **2026-10-09 menu fixes.** Old theme panel paper is `public/images/old-panel-paper-v3.webp` (Gaymi's image; it came with a painted-in checkerboard, cut out by flood-filling the grey/white from the edges); its red X (`.panel-box > .panel-close-btn`) is moved inside the paper (top 52px / right 82px) - at the Golden spot it sat on the see-through torn corner. `#play-btn` width is `--play-w` (set per height tier) and `.play-btn-centered` uses that width plus `height: calc(var(--play-w) * 467 / 1400)` - never `fit-content`: the button's own text is transparent (the plate picture says PLAY), so fit-content shrank it to the hidden text. The right column scales with CSS `zoom` (`fitRightColumn` in index.html's `window.__menuLayout`, see the first-paint note below): the smaller of on-screen height room and `innerWidth / FULL_WIDTH`, floored at `MIN_ZOOM`; when the column is taller than the window it switches to `flex-start` (safe center left the last buttons below the screen on phones). The Old theme X sits in the middle of the paper's curled top-right flap (`top: calc(8.7% - 18px); right: calc(6.2% - 18px)` for v3 - "the middle of that corner of the old paper"; a first try on flat paper left of the curl was the wrong reading). The right column is 260px (was 240, "Achievements" ran under the plaque's end cap) with the Old card at `calc(100% + 24px)`, and `fitNavLabels` (in `__menuLayout`) shrinks any label that would cross the last 15% of its plaque (`NAV_CAP`, down to 70%) - long Spanish/Hindi names. Settings checkboxes (`.audio-checkbox`) are plain color since 2026-10-10 ("only colour, not something in it" - the riveted plate with a brushed check was dropped): `--cb-off` fill inside a `--cb-rim` border, solid `--cb-on` when checked, nothing drawn inside; gold in Golden, brown under `html.ui-theme-old`. Nav order (same day): General, Store, Market, Upgrades, Global, **Friend List** (renamed from Friends), Quests, Inventory, Achievements, **Gallery** (Coming Soon - a toast; `#gallery-btn`, always right after Achievements like Market after Store), Map Editor. `NAV_ORDER_DEFAULT`/`_loadNavOrder` in Game.js (and index.html's nav anti-flash script) swap a save still holding the old default for the new one; a custom order is kept. Clans: the Clan tab always opens on Clans (`clan-subtab-ranking` click), `_fetchClanRanking` caches the list for `CLAN_RANKING_CACHE_MS` and is started when General opens (dropped whenever `settings.clanId` changes), and "Your Clan" comes before the kill count.

- **Old theme panels are torn paper, not black boxes (2026-10-03, Gaymi's own image - since 2026-10-04 `public/images/old-panel-paper-clean.webp`, a stain-free sheet "to look more clean", already transparent around the torn edges, cropped to the paper).** `html.ui-theme-old .panel-box` stretches it as the background (no border/box-shadow, a `drop-shadow` filter that follows the torn edge, extra padding so content stays off the rim) and one rule turns all text on it dark ink (`--old-ink`, `!important` since most panel text has ID-level colors). Anything on its own dark background keeps its light text via that rule's `:not(:is(...))` list (`.crate-card`, `.inv-skin-card`, `#inv-skin-preview-level`, `.market-filter-btn`, `#shop-skin-buy-btn`, the close X - `#inv-skin-preview` itself left the list on 2026-10-10 when its dark box was removed) - a new dark card inside a panel needs adding there, or its text turns dark on dark. Golden theme is untouched.

- **Old theme profile card (`#menu-player-badge`, 2026-10-03, from Gaymi's mockup)** is a rusty riveted metal plate (`public/images/old-profile-plate.webp`, cut from the mockup with its baked-in ID and level painted out) at a fixed 1230:1278 aspect, 264px wide - the same width as the nav plaques under it (they hang 12px past the column each side, so the card does too via `margin: 0 -12px`; asked for 2026-10-04 "same width as the right side panel... bigger so it wouldn't look weird"), its ID/level text sized in `cqw` so it scales with the card; still 0px homepage overflow at 1920x1080/1600x900/1440x900/1366x768; the face window, the level coin (`old-level-coin.webp`, its own picture so it sits on top of the face) and the ID name plate are absolutely positioned by percentages of that picture - change the picture and those percentages move with it. Golden keeps its gold frame and star. The face gets an aged look for any skin (2026-10-04): a sepia/darken `filter` on `#menu-avatar-photo` plus `#menu-avatar-icon::after` laying `old-face-wear.webp` on top (rust flakes, scratches, cracks and grain lifted from the mockup's face, transparent elsewhere); the ID plate's inside is plate metal now, not the dark patch first painted over the mockup's text ("remove the black around the id").

- **Every menu panel and every tab inside one is the same height (standing rule, 2026-10-04, Gaymi: "dont make the panel behind change sizes ... add this to your memory").** Switching Achievements from Zombie Survival to Deathmatch shrank the Old theme's paper ~120px, and Levels/Terms/Settings were all slightly different heights. The shared `#...-panel .panel-box` width rule in `style.css` also sets `min-height: calc(68vh + var(--panel-chrome))` - every panel's content area is 68vh, and `--panel-chrome` (138px Golden, 224px Old, on `:root`/`html.ui-theme-old`) is the title + padding around it, sized to the tallest panel (Achievements). A new panel goes in that selector list; a new tab or panel whose content is shorter must not shrink the box, and one that needs more room scrolls inside its 68vh area rather than growing the box. Check by opening every `_routes` entry and clicking every tab at 768/1010/1200px tall and comparing `.panel-box` `offsetHeight` - all must match, in both themes.

- **No panel ever scrolls sideways (standing rule, 2026-10-04, Gaymi: "you cant scroll right to left on any panel on gayz").** Game Mode had a left-right scrollbar because `.mode-toggle`'s `margin: 0 -7px` hover bleed made its rows 7px wider than the page; inside panels it's `margin: 0` now, and the shared scroll wrappers (`.panel-fixed-content`, `.hub-tab-page`, `.general-tab-page`) are `overflow-x: hidden`. Anything new inside a panel must fit its width (wrap text, `min-width: 0` on flex children, no negative side margins) - `tests/panels.spec.js` checks every element in every panel and tab, in both themes, for `scrollWidth > clientWidth` with `overflow-x` auto/scroll.

- **Old theme crate cards and Market menus are not black (2026-10-04, Gaymi: "make the crates not black, use the colour around them" / "black is not seeable"):** in the Old theme each `.crate-card` is filled with a gradient of its own `--crate-tier-color` (wood brown, ice blue, golden, monthly purple) with dark-ink names and a dark-brown price pill, and the Market's All items / All rarities filters (`.market-filter-btn` / `.market-filter-menu`) are parchment with dark ink and a brown edge - their options had been turned dark-on-black by the panel's dark-ink rule. Any new dropdown/menu inside an Old theme panel needs the same parchment treatment (or an entry in that rule's `:not()` list).

- **Old theme profile face is dimmed, not washed out (2026-10-04, "not so bright"):** `#menu-avatar-photo` is `brightness(0.72)` with sepia, the plate itself `brightness(0.88) contrast(1.1)`, and the face-wear overlay is drawn twice (second copy scaled/shifted) with `mix-blend-mode: multiply` - laid on normally, `old-face-wear.webp`'s light grain washed the face out.

- **Design a Skin has no page background in the Old theme (2026-10-04):** `public/skin-designer/index.html` makes `html`/`body` transparent under `:root[data-theme="old"]` so the paper panel shows through the iframe, and the text that sits straight on the paper (title, intro, Slot label, Weapon note) uses the dark ink; the tool cards keep their dark fill.

- **Nav buttons are the same width/clearance in both themes (2026-10-04):** Golden used to sit at the column width with 16px left padding, so its icons and words landed on the gold plate's end dots; `html:not(.ui-theme-old) #menu-nav-buttons button` now copies Old's `margin: 0 -12px` and left padding at every height tier.

## Biome look in games (2026-10-25)

TopBar sets `--biome-ground`, `--biome-bg-rgb` (the picture's average, pushed away from its ink) and `--biome-ink-rgb` (CleanTheme `oppositeInk`, like Simple) on <html> - BiomeBar `coloursOf`. 2026-10-26 readability fix: the old dark wash + white text + per-biome `ACCENT` was unreadable on mid tones (badlands); the accent now equals the ink. `settings.playBiomeLook` (General > Theme toggle, default on) switches it off. `BuildMode.exit` turns it off so the homepage never keeps it (it used to stick after leaving). This is Gaymi's deliberate exception to "everything follows the theme": in-game screens follow the biome, homepage follows the theme. New in-game UI: add it to play-biome.css. Chat (all text, Global/Clan tabs) and hotbar numbers/selected border are always black with a soft white glow (`--black-text-edge`, style.css), in every theme and biome.
