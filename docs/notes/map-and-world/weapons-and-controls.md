# Weapons, aiming, controls, camera

Split out of the old `map-and-world.md` on 2026-11-13 (word for word, oldest first). Add new notes at the end.

- **Phone controls (2026-10-06, "make it simple, icons not words") - `BuildTouch.js` (`buildMode.touch`).** Only on touch devices (`isTouchDevice`: hover none + coarse pointer) and only while walking the map (`tryMode.active` - Map 1 Play and Try Map; building stays keyboard/mouse). One full-screen `#touch-play` overlay: a floating stick in the left 45% (`_touchMove` {x, y, run}, read by `BuildTryMode.update` as analog movement - part-way pushes walk slower, past `RUN_AT` runs, up/down also climbs ladders), drag anywhere else to look (Mouse Sensitivity/Invert Look apply), and SVG icon buttons with no text: Fire (hold repeats every `FIRE_REPEAT_S`), Jump (holds `Space` in `_keys`), Reload, Use (`_tryUseFromCamera` - doors, chests, NPCs), Crouch (toggles `KeyC`), Pause (`toggleMenu`). Everything goes through the keyboard/mouse paths, so behavior can't differ. The buttons hide under the pause menu, an NPC panel or game over; `body.touch-play-on` (whole time you're in the map) hides the hotbar/chat/tool hint, shrinks the minimap and moves ammo, wave line and the fps readout out of the thumbs' way. Test with `isMobile`/`hasTouch` and CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent` (tests/buildmode.spec.js "on a phone") - `touchStart` with several points re-sends earlier fingers as new, so test one finger at a time.

- **Weapon models are real Sketchfab models (2026-10-10, each picked by Gaymi).** 15 weapons, one GLB each in `public/models/weapons/<weaponId>.glb` (all CC BY 4.0 - makers in `CREDITS.txt` there and the Credits page's `#credits-weapons-list`; a new model needs both). `Viewmodels.js` is now only `preloadWeaponModels()` + `buildViewmodel()` driven by `WEAPON_MODELS` (`rot` quarter turns so the muzzle points -z, `len` in game units, optional `tilt` for blades, `pose` for the crossbow's skinned limbs, `paint` for the colorless Void Ripper); the Quaternius pack and every procedural gun were deleted. **Copy a viewmodel with `cloneViewmodel()` (SkeletonUtils), never `clone(true)`** - a plain clone leaves the crossbow's skinned limbs/string floating. Files go through a gltf-transform pipeline before committing (dedup/flatten/join, base color 512px + other maps 256px webp, meshopt; drop extra nodes - the AK file has gold/worn copies stacked on it, the M1911 a loose mag): ~5 MB on disk, ~57 MB texture memory for all 15 (exception to the 256px rule below, budgeted). Weapons: the Glock 18 became the Revolver, the GPMG and Tomahawk were added (`PLAY_WEAPONS`, `melee: true` marks blades for melee kills), Nail Gun/Harpoon Gun deleted; old saves holding a removed id fall back on load. Sketchfab and GitHub release-asset hosts are unreachable from the sandbox - the owner downloads and uploads models; a *published* release's `github.com/.../releases/download/...` URL works with curl (drafts don't), and this session can't delete releases (403).

- **Settings > Controls = Map 1's keys only (2026-10-10, Gaymi: "delete all of these").** `ACTIONS` (Keybinds.js) is just moveForward/Back/Left/Right, jump, sprint (Shift), crouch, reload, use (E - doors/chests/NPCs, replaced `FIXED_KEYS.buildUse`), inspectWeapon, cycleMap; ~35 old-Map-1 actions and `HOTBAR_ITEM_SLOTS` were deleted with their i18n keys. Try Map reads them through `heldAction(keys, id)` (binding plus free fallbacks: arrows for movement, Right Shift, Ctrl/Caps Lock for crouch) - never check a raw `KeyW`/`Space`/`KeyC` there again; the editor's free-fly camera and its tool letters stay fixed. A save whose sprint is the old default E gets Shift back (`loadBindings`). **No "Loading Map Editor..." screen and no freeze on Play (2026-10-10, "it lags then loads"):** after boot, idle slices (`requestIdleCallback` *with a timeout* - the homepage rain/water redraw every frame, so without one they never ran) fetch BuildMode.js (`_preloadBuildMode()`), and construct `this.buildMode` (generating Map 1 there too - `_map3Base` - made the two-player server test drop a page in the full suite, so the city is still generated on Play). The weapon pictures (`_weaponThumbnailsReady()`) are drawn one gun per break as `toBlob` blob URLs, started the first time the picker/Inventory needs them and paused while a game runs with the picker closed - 15 synchronous `toDataURL` readbacks were most of the freeze; cards fill in as they finish. Drawing them at page load instead made the two-player server test drop a page (a busy GPU stalls the other page's syncs past `STALE_MS`). `BlockChunks._glowSourcesNear` gathers a chunk's glow sources once per build instead of 27 map lookups per vertex. Measured (software GL): Play 18s -> 0.7s, first frame 2.9s -> 1.7s. `_enterBuildMode` awaits it first and hides `#menu` only once the map is entered (a second click meanwhile is ignored, `_enteringBuildMode`).

- **Aim and Inspect (2026-10-10, from Kirka screenshots).** In Try Map/Play, right-click toggles aiming down the sights (since 2026-10-10 a toggle, not held - "if you stop right clicking it stops aiming"; `BuildMode._onPointerDown` -> `tryMode.setAim(!aiming)`, opening the pause menu turns it off; `BuildTryMode.aimAmount` eases 0..1): the gun slides from `GUN_OFFSET` to a per-gun aim spot in the middle with less bob (`_aimOffset`, set in `_findHandSpots` from the gun's exact vertex box - `setFromObject(gun, true)`, since turned parts' own box corners stick up past the gun: its back `AIM_BACK` + `AIM_BACK_PER_LEN`·length in front of the eye (negative for long guns - the eye sits over the stock), its highest point on the sight line through the crosshair (`AIM_DROP` tiny) and then `AIM_SIGHT_RISE` higher so the crosshair sits inside the sights/scope (`AIM_BACK_FOR` holds sightless guns - the Minigun - further out) - 2026-10-10 "the aim looks weird", then "not in the middle, does not look like aiming" after a first try that held it below the crosshair bloxd-style; a fixed spot stretched every gun from the bottom of the screen; a model whose barrel isn't straight gets a `yaw` in `WEAPON_MODELS`, like the AK), the view zooms in `AIM_ZOOM` degrees (no sprint widening while aiming), mouse look turns 45% slower, and spread weapons tighten (`BuildSurvival.shoot`). Right-click no longer uses doors/chests/NPCs in Try Map - that's only E (`FIXED_KEYS.buildUse`). **Inspect Weapon** is a rebindable action (`inspectWeapon`, X - it replaced the old Map 1's dead `grapple` action): the gun turns its side toward you at an angle (`_inspectWeight`, `INSPECT_IN/HOLD/OUT`), cancelled by firing or aiming; it's on the pause screen's Shortcuts (an entry whose keys are a function, so a rebind shows) with a check in `tests/docs.spec.js`.

- **Camera views, big map tiles, aim centring (2026-10-13 batch).** The `cameraView` action (P, rebindable, on the pause Shortcuts with a docs.spec check) cycles `tryMode.view` 0 first / 1 behind / 2 in front (`cycleView`); third person draws your own body (`_updateBody`/`_buildBody`, built from the arms' skin `_armSkin`) holding your gun through **`HeldWeapon.js`** (`holdWeapon`/`poseArms`, shared with PlayNet's other players), and `applyView()` moves the camera out (up to `VIEW_DIST`, stopped by blocks, `VIEW_RISE` over your head from behind, `lookAt` your eyes from the front) **only around `renderer.render` in `BuildMode.render`** and restores it - shooting, doors and every other ray still use your eyes; no aiming or first-person gun in third person. Big map: tiles not drawn yet show `OpenWorld.mapTileCoarse` (8x8 samples) at once, detailed tiles get `MAP_TILE_BUDGET_MS` per draw nearest the middle first, and `_freeMouseForMap` releases pointer lock whenever the big map is open. Aim: the crosshair centres on the gun's top band (`_topLineX`, `TOP_BAND`) instead of its whole box (the AK's side parts pulled it off), and `AIM_LOWER_FOR` drops the M1911 a little.

Mag upgrades add a third of the gun's own magazine; chest/Trader ammo goes through `ammoFor` (written for the rifle). Try Map's gun model follows the pick (`BuildTryMode._showGun`). Game Mode has Parkour and Custom tabs after Deathmatch, Achievements a Parkour tab (all Coming Soon; Gaymi: no Custom tab in Achievements - `_renderAchievementsPanel` shows its placeholder for any mode but `survival`). **Achievements added the same day** (10, `Achievements.js`): each is read from what a run reports in `_onPlayEvent` - the `'kill'` report carries `weapon` (Arsenal, `ARSENAL_KEY` keeps every gun with a kill) and `boss`; `'end'` carries `headshots`/`meleeKills`/`chests`/`upgrades` (sum of camp upgrade levels)/`coins`/`seconds`/`mode`; goals are the `*_HEADSHOTS`/`*_WAVES`/... constants next to `MEAT_GRINDER_STREAK`. - **Clean crisp blocks and guns, hold-to-aim, deeper zoom, breaking blocks, gameplay pause menu (2026-10-17, Gaymi: "make the blocks more clearer instead of so burry like kirka and bloxd ... same with the weapon ... low ploy but stilll clean, clear and not blurry and simple with low lag ... when you hold right click it aims ... if you stop clicking right click it stops aiming ... make them all breakable but with speed ... stone should take longer to break compared to wood ... pressing v in game play ... zoom inn further ... when pressing escape ... back to menu, settings, save progress, and leave game ... dont make that map editor pop up thats only for when making maps").** *Textures:* **the HD pass of 2026-10-16 is gone** (`BlockTexturesHD.js`, its worker, `prepareHdTextures`/`hdCanvasFor`/`_applyHdTextures`/`refreshAtlas` deleted - the note above about it is history). Every 16x16 face from `BlockTextures.js` goes through `cleanPixels` (`PixelClean.js`: k-means to `CLEAN_COLORS` = 4 flat colours, then a pixel with at most one same-colour neighbour takes the commonest colour of its 4 neighbours; the picture tiles round) in `baseFaces` - not water/lava, not glass/honey/slime (leaves yes, alpha kept). The chunk atlas uses **16px tiles** (`tile: 16`, 2048 wide, ~2 MB) with `magFilter = NearestFilter`; `_canvasTexture` (slabs, stairs, doors, game blocks) and the liquid maps are nearest too. *Guns:* `Viewmodels.simplifyMaterials` runs once per GLB in `preloadWeaponModels` (not the painted Void Ripper): each material's base colour is drawn at `GUN_TEX_SIZE` (128), darkened by how metal each pixel is (glTF metal/rough picture's blue channel x `metalness` - metal relied on reflections and went white without them), cut to `GUN_COLORS` (8) with `cleanPixels`, nearest filtering; normal/roughness/metalness/ao maps dropped, roughness 0.65, metalness 0.1, the original pictures closed/disposed. *Aim:* `BuildMode._onPointerDown` button 2 -> `setAim(true)`, `_onPointerUp` button 2 -> `setAim(false)` (it was a toggle since 2026-10-10). *Zoom:* while trying/playing `BuildTryMode.update` owns the FOV and damps to `PLAY_ZOOM_FOV` (9) while the zoom key is held; `BuildMode.update`'s own `ZOOM_FOV` damping only runs when not trying (the two used to fight, so the zoom stopped part way). *Breaking:* `BuildTryMode._updateDigging` (every frame from `update`): while `_fireHeld` and no menu/picker/death, the block under the crosshair within `DIG_REACH` (5) gains time; at `BuildMode.breakSeconds(type)` it breaks (`breakBlock`), a crack (`_digMesh`, one box, opacity by progress) shows meanwhile, looking elsewhere starts over. `breakSeconds`: `BREAK_SECONDS` by picker category plus `BREAK_SECONDS_BY_ID` overrides (bedrock 10, obsidian 7, leaves/flowers 0.25, wool/snow 0.5, concrete 1.6, C4 0.5); shaped blocks use their source block (`SHAPED_TEXTURE_FROM`/`slabOf`/`stairsOf`), doors/ladders/fences wood; game blocks, invisible and liquids Infinity; a door's top half breaks the door from its bottom. Broken blocks go in the same `_broken` map as shot-out windows (now with `state` from `_blockStateOf` and `terrain` for open-world land), so `exit()`/`_restoreWindows` puts them all back (land goes back into `terrainKeys`, out of `removed`) and `_snapshot` saves them as present (land excepted, door/facing state kept). Shards: solid blocks get opaque shards in their colour (`BREAK_LOOKS` 'chunks'). Not synced to other players on a server. *Pause menu:* `PlayPauseMenu.js` + `#play-pause-menu` (index.html) + `src/play-pause.css` (imported in main.js; Golden dark/gold, Old torn paper/Rye). `toggleMenu` still does the pausing (`menuOpen`), but when `gameMenu.forGame` (survival active and `fromMenu`) it shows this instead of `#build-menu`. ## 2026-10-17: Switch Weapon and special gun powers

- Pause menu **Switch Weapon** (`PlayPauseMenu.switchWeapon`) closes the pause screen and opens the weapon picker with `_openWeaponPicker(true)`; the run stays frozen while `_picking` (single player). `_pickWeapon` stores the old gun's `{mag, reserve}` in `_ammoByWeapon` and restores it for a gun used before (saved with the run), so switching never refills. Knives skip this.
- **Powers** are fields in `PLAY_WEAPONS`: `burn` (Flamethrower, seconds; `BURN_DPS` in `_updateZombie`), `pierce` (Crossbow 3, AWP 4 - zombies on the ray, nearest first), `slow` (Void Ripper, `VOID_SLOW`), `cleave` (Tomahawk - cone `CLEAVE_COS` within reach), `blast` (launchers). Host/single player only for burn/slow (server clients' mirrored zombies don't burn).
- **Effects:** `PlayFx.js` - one pool of world sprites (no lights): `explode`, `flameStream`, `burn`, `voidHit`, updated from `BuildSurvival.update`. First-person: `GunFx.flame` (Flamethrower nozzle fire) and a purple flash tint for the Void Ripper.

## Real-world M map, worn guns, Coming Soon guns, GPMG (2026-10-18, third batch)

- **EarthAtlas.js**: World Map mode only (`isEarthWorld()`). BuildTryMode `_drawMap` draws `drawEarthAtlas` instead of block tiles when the centre map's span is over `ATLAS_FROM` (960 = the block map's own `MAP_MAX_SPAN`); the centre map opens at `ATLAS_OPEN_SPAN` (30 degrees) and zooms out to `ATLAS_MAX_SPAN` (whole Earth); pan limit is half of that. `#build-try-map.atlas` makes it opaque (the centre map is 0.85 otherwise). The atlas picture (360x180 degrees x PX=8, ~16 MB canvas) is built once on first use from the half-degree country grid: land colours blurred among land cells, mask in alpha, scaled up smoothly then thresholded at 0.5 so coasts are curved and crisp; borders are dashed half-degree edges. Country names sit at the middle of each country's biggest piece (flood fill). Coloured from biomes but `beach`/`snowyBeach` are greens (the UK coast cells are `snowyBeach`).
- **Worn guns** (`Viewmodels.js` `wearPixels`): after `cleanPixels`, each gun colour picture is scaled to `WEAR_SIZE` 256 (nearest) and gets fade, edge scuffs, scratches, grime and rust, seeded. At 128 the scratches showed as big white blotches on the GPMG up close (low UV density). Void Ripper is `paint` (no picture) so it has no wear.
- **Coming Soon guns**: `COMING_SOON_WEAPONS` / `canPlayWeapon` in PlayWeapons.js - picker cards disabled with `.weapon-soon-tag`, Inventory > Weapons cards greyed with no `data-weapon-card` (no menu), saved runs/inventories swap them for `DEFAULT_PLAY_WEAPON`. GayZ Features "More Weapons" + docs.spec MAP line.
- **GPMG**: bipod (12 pieces), side ammo box with belt (4) and a floating piece (3) removed from `gpmg.glb` by connected component with @gltf-transform (meshopt kept), material `alphaMode` OPAQUE (was BLEND = see-through). Weapon thumbs regenerated.

## Gun finishes, hand light, varied cracks (2026-10-18, sixth batch)

- Viewmodels.js: `gunmetalPixels(canvas, finish)` repaints only metal pixels (`isMetal`: saturation < METAL_SAT or near black) - wood/paint keep their colours. `FINISH[id]` = tint, mean, contrast, roughness, metalness per gun (DEFAULT_FINISH = GPMG). Colours GUN_COLORS 10, wear at WEAR_SIZE 384 (counts scale with size). 192px colour pictures cost ~+450ms page load - don't.
- `addGunLights(scene, keyAt, rimAt, strength)` is the one gun light: thumbnails and the hand's gun view (HAND_LIGHT 0.85). The hand's gun view renders with NoToneMapping - the world's ACES tone mapping greyed the gun's colours versus its picture.
- Digging: `_showDigCrack` picks one of DIG_LOOKS patterns per dig, a random quarter-turn rotation, and DIG_STAGES growth stages (`_crackTexture(look, amount, colors)` draws segments nearest the impact first; dark lines on solid blocks). Breaking picks a random BREAK_LOOKS burst.

- **Snappier walking + heavy guns (2026-11-13, "feels like there's a rock on you").** `GROUND_GRIP` 15 -> 20, new `STOP_GRIP` 30 when no move key is held (on the ground). `PLAY_WEAPONS[id].heavy` (= `HEAVY` 0.85: minigun, gpmg, flamethrower, rocket, launcher) scales speed in `_walk` while the gun is in hand (not a block/item). Measured on the Mac at 60 fps: full speed in ~0.15 s; the ~1 s "freezes" seen while testing were the hidden browser pane throttling, not the game.

## Ambient cues (AmbientCues.js, 2026-11-14)
`BuildTryMode.update` calls `tickAmbientCues` ~10x/s (also in Try mode). Indoors = any solid cell 2-13 blocks above the player's feet → `Audio.updateAmbientZone` (duller wind). Horde = survival zombies within 28 blocks, closer ones weigh more, panned to the nearest → `Audio.updateZombiePresence`, scaled to 60% and by the Zombie slider (Gaymi chose "subtle"). `exit()` resets both. Not wired: footstep muffling indoors (`playFootstep(muffled)`), riding vehicles skips nothing (tick runs before the ride code).
