# Map Editor, share codes, Community Maps

Split out of the old `map-and-world.md` on 2026-11-13 (word for word, oldest first). Add new notes at the end.

- **Map editor blocks are chunk meshes, not one InstancedMesh per type (since 2026-09-29).** `BlockChunks.js` cuts the world into 16x16x16 chunks and meshes only faces touching air/see-through blocks, with Minecraft-style per-vertex corner shading, all block textures in one atlas (so one draw call per chunk and material kind). Measured in the software renderer: ~1450ms -> ~60ms per frame, and ~200k -> ~32k triangles for the default floor. `BuildMode.placeBlock`/`removeBlock` only update `_blocks`/`_chunkCells` and mark chunks dirty; `render()` rebuilds dirty chunks once per frame. Only shaped blocks (stairs/fence/ladder) still use `_instancedMeshes`. Ladders hang flat against the first solid neighbor (`_ladderMatrix`, `LADDER_SIDES`; re-checked after a bulk load and whenever a neighbor is placed/broken) instead of standing in the middle of their cell (2026-10-02 report: "looks weird"). The editor has **no point lights at all** (since 2026-09-30): glowing blocks light their surroundings through baked vertex light in the chunk meshes (`BlockChunks.setGlow`/`_glowAt`, Minecraft-style block light, rebuilt with the chunks in its radius) - the 6 light proxies it had before cost ~35% of every editor frame even with nothing glowing nearby. Don't add real lights to the editor scene. Shaped blocks use MeshLambertMaterial like the chunks. The editor auto-lowers resolution to hold 60 fps (or the FPS Cap) (`_updateEditorResScale` in Game.js, Auto Quality setting): Retina extra pixels go first, and only a device still slow at 1 pixel per CSS pixel goes lower, floored at `EDITOR_MIN_PIXEL_RATIO` (0.6). With the Shadows setting on, the editor's shadow map is redrawn only when chunks rebuild (`_shadowsDirty` in `BuildMode.render()`, `shadowMap.autoUpdate` off just for that render) - the sun never moves. MSAA is off on high-DPI touch devices (`highDpiTouch` in Game.js). The editor renders without the survival game's ACES tone mapping. Two old bugs fixed on the way: `_rgb()` wrote THREE.Color's *linear* components into canvas fillStyles, drawing every block texture ~3x too dark (use `getHex()`/`getStyle()` for canvas colors - `new THREE.Color(hex).r` is linear, not the hex's sRGB value), and `getImageData` on a GPU-backed canvas made the editor take ~7s to open (texture canvases use `willReadFrequently: true`). Block textures are Minecraft-style 16x16 pixel art painted procedurally by `BlockTextures.js` (seeded per block id, per-face top/side/bottom for grass/logs/bookshelf/TNT/pumpkin/hay/cactus/sandstone/bone); `blockIconURL()` draws the isometric inventory icons the picker and hotbar show. A new block type falls back to its `pattern` name there - give it a proper painter in `BY_ID`/`OVERRIDE` rather than relying on the fallback. **Water and lava flow like Minecraft (`LiquidFlow.js`, `buildMode.liquids`, 2026-10-03):** a placed block is a source; every water tick (0.25s) / lava tick (1.5s) liquid falls first, then spreads sideways (water 7, lava 3 via step 2) toward the nearest drop within 4/2 cells, dries up when nothing feeds it, two water sources make a third, and lava touching water becomes obsidian (source) / cobblestone (flowing). Flowing cells are ordinary `water`/`lava` entries in `_blocks` with their level in `liquids.levels` (`FALLING` = 99 - it once was 8, which a level-7 neighbor + 1 also produced, so drained water turned into undying "falling" cells); `_snapshot()` skips them (sources re-flow on load), the raycast passes through them, and the `placeBlock`/`removeBlock` wrappers (real work in `_placeBlockInner`/`_removeBlockInner`) replace a flow cell you build into and call `liquids.changed()`. The mesher slopes the surface by averaging corner heights (`_liquidCorners`, `liquidHeight` option). Water got bloxd.io's lighter, clearer blue, and in Try Map a head under water/lava tints the screen (`#build-try-underwater`). Try Map also drops the camera's near plane to 0.012 (`NEAR`, restored on exit) - at the flying camera's 0.1 (a third of a block) standing against a wall showed straight through it. Water/lava (`LIQUIDS` in `BlockChunks.js`) have their own scrolling textures (`animate()`, called from `BuildMode.render()`), a surface 2px below the cell top, never hide neighbors' faces, and don't block movement (`_blockedAt` skips them). Slabs are `shape: 'slab'` (half-height `CUSTOM_BLOCK_GEOMETRY.slab`), textured from the full block named in `SHAPED_TEXTURE_FROM`. Every full block also has a generated slab (`${id}slab`, `slabOf` = source id; generated right after `BLOCK_TYPES`), textured top/side/bottom from its source. Shaped blocks' InstancedMeshes are created lazily on first placement and grown by doubling (`_shapedMesh`) - never pre-allocate one per shaped type (with ~190 of them that was ~270 MB). Doors (2026-09-30, `shape: 'door'`) are two cells tall: the bottom is the picker type, the top is a hidden generated `${id}top` type (`hidden: true`, left out of `PICKER_TYPES`) placed/removed only together with it (`placeBlock`/`removeBlock`), each half showing its half of the 16x32 `doorCanvas`. Facing/open state lives in `_doorState` (both halves' keys), goes into the instance matrix (`_doorMatrix`), is saved as `facing`/`open` on the bottom entry (top halves aren't saved), and rides along in undo entries (`state`). Right-click on a door toggles it (`toggleDoor`) instead of placing; open doors don't block movement. TNT was replaced by C4 the same day - `LEGACY_TYPE_IDS` maps old saves' `tnt`/`tntslab` to `c4`/`c4slab`; add any future renamed/removed block id there rather than letting old builds silently drop it. **Saved builds are trusted as-is (fixed 2026-10-01):** `_applyBuild` only fills the grass ground layer for a slot with no ground in its data (never saved, or old/shared data without one) - it used to run `_ensureGroundLayer()` on every load, which put grass back over every cell the player had broken. Edits also autosave 1.5s after the last change (`_scheduleAutosave`, hooked into `_recordUndo`/undo/redo/`toggleDoor`) and on `pagehide`/tab hide - before, only Exit/Save/slot switch saved, so closing the tab lost the build. `enter({ slot })` clears the scene when the slot changes (the scene otherwise keeps the last build between visits). **Map 3** (Game Mode > Map, and a `Map 3` button after the slot buttons - the slot row `#build-slots` lives inside the Escape menu `#build-menu` since 2026-10-02, and the editor's fps readout took its old top-left spot; since the same day `#build-menu` is a full-screen GayZ dark/gold **pause screen**: title, a preview card (a `toDataURL` of the canvas taken right after a render in `_captureMenuPreview`), the action buttons with icons in the middle, and Quick Info / Shortcuts / Tip cards on the right, filled by `_refreshMenuInfo`; its colors and title font come from `--bm-gold`/`--bm-gold-rgb`/`--bm-panel`/`--bm-text`/`--bm-title-font` on `#build-menu`, overridden under `html.ui-theme-old` with the Old theme's bronze and Rye font - never hard-code gold rgba values in it again - its Shortcuts list (`MENU_SHORTCUTS`) must only list keys that really work, incl. the Ctrl/Cmd+Z/Y/S handlers added for it; last-saved times live in `localStorage['buildmode-saved-at']`. Button labels are inner `<span id="build-mode-*-btn-label">`s - `SIMPLE_TEXT_I18N_KEYS` targets those, never the buttons, or the icons get wiped) is a ready-made block city from `Map3Generator.js` (seeded, deterministic, only existing block ids, ~42k blocks) opened as slot `'map3'`; since the map regenerates identically, only the player's changes are stored, as a `{removed, placed}` diff in `localStorage['buildmode-map3-edits']` (deliberately not a `gayz-` key, so it never bloats Cloud Save). Changing the generator changes Map 3 for everyone and can misplace existing diffs - bump the key name if you change it substantially. Reset Map on Map 3 restores the generated city. **Invisible Block** (`invisible`, `shape: 'invisible'`): a solid cube whose material has `colorWrite` off, so it draws nothing but still casts real sun shadows (Shadows setting); since that setting is off by default it also gets its own straight-down shadow square on the first block below it (`_rebuildInvisibleShadows`, one InstancedMesh, hidden while real shadows are on), and shows as a faint ghost while held (`_updateInvisibleGhost`). Map 3's edits key is `buildmode-map3-v7-edits` (bumped on every map change - v7 for the staircases; except the 2026-10-03 **crates**: 60 empty `crate` blocks (own `crate` painter in `BlockTextures.js`) dropped at street level by their own seeded RNG as the generator's very last step, so nothing else moved and saved edits still line up; kept off doorways, ladder fronts and narrow gaps, re-checked 73/73 buildings reachable and 0 wall-less ladders). **While building (not trying), both arms made from the equipped skin hold the selected block between them, a little right of the middle of the screen** (went arm -> just the block, "like minecraft nothing else" -> one arm holding it -> both arms in the middle, from the user's own mockup, then hands moved in onto the block's sides - "not even touching" - all 2026-10-03; `HAND_ARM` lists both limbs, same `ARM_PX` width as Try Map's arms) (`BuildTryMode.drawHand`, drawn on top in Try Map's gun scene; the block's parts come from `BuildMode._heldBlockParts` - a textured cube, or a shaped block's own mesh; it swings on every place/break via `swingHand()`; hidden while the pause menu or picker is open; `HAND_BLOCK_POS`/`HAND_BLOCK_SIZE` place it). A loaded hotbar also re-selects its highlighted slot (`selectedType = hotbar[activeHotbarIndex]` in `_applyParsedData`) - before, opening the editor left you empty-handed (nothing held, clicks placed nothing) until you switched slots. **Save slots (since 2026-10-02): 10 of them, each its own device-only entry `localStorage['buildmode-slot-N']` in a compact run-length format (`_encodeSlot`/`_decodeSlot`: palette + `y,z,x,len,p` runs + a separate door list).** The old single `gayz-build-mode-slots` array (plain block lists, ~650 KB per map) overran localStorage with a few maps and Cloud Save's 1 MB doc; `_migrateSlots` moves it (and the even older `gayz-build-mode`) over once and removes it - builds no longer sync between devices (Export/Import does). **Try Map** (`BuildTryMode.js`, T key or the pause screen's first button): first-person walking with gravity and real collision in Minecraft sizes (0.6 wide x 1.8 tall, eyes 1.62, 0.55 step-up - 1.05 onto stairs, so staircases walk up - ~1.3 jump, ladders climb, liquids swim, open doors pass; slabs/stairs count as half-height via `BuildMode._cellSolidTop`) and a **bloxd.io feel** (asked for by name 2026-10-02, then "exactly like bloxd" 2026-10-03, so bloxd's own numbers): walk 4 / run 7 (Shift + W only - the double-tap W latch was removed) / crouch 2 blocks/s (C, Ctrl, Z or Caps Lock: 1.5 tall, eyes 1.27, won't walk off an edge deeper than a step, stands back up only with room overhead), bloxd's bunny hop (a *fresh* Space press within `BHOP_WINDOW` 0.12s of landing after a jump adds a link, `BHOP_MULT` 1 / 1.15 / 1.225 / 1.3; staying on the ground longer or a late/coyote jump resets it; holding Space jumps again on every landing and counts - it used to set a one-frame jump buffer that the same frame's countdown zeroed, so holding Space never hopped at all), near-instant start/stop (`GROUND_GRIP`), strong air steering (`AIR_GRIP`), coyote time + jump buffer, holding Space keeps hopping, sprint widens the FOV by 8 (restored on exit), almost no head bob. Right-click or E opens/closes the door you look at within 5 blocks (`_tryUseFromCamera`, which calls `camera.updateMatrixWorld()` first and re-closes a door that would shut onto the player), held inside the 128x128 ground; the gun is a clone of the player's first hotbar gun drawn in its own scene/camera after a `clearDepth()` (`drawGun`), so it never clips into walls, held by Kirka-style blocky arms built from the equipped skin (`settings.customSkinDataUrl` or the default; `buildTexturedCharacter`'s `limbPivots.armR/armL`, stretched from `ARM_SHOULDERS` to each gun's own hand spots - `_findHandSpots` measures the gun's mesh bounds, right hand on the grip near the back, left hand under the barrel halfway along (the viewmodels' hidden `isHand` grip markers sit in the wrong places for this, so they're ignored); `ARM_PX` 0.036, widened 2026-10-03 from 0.026 ("too skinny"); Lambert-toned since the gun scene's lights are made for metal), with the editor's hotbar still at the bottom but drawn empty (`_renderHotbar` skips icons while `tryMode.active`; the old controls hint box `#build-try-hint` was removed 2026-10-03 at Gaymi's request - the pause screen's Shortcuts list covers the keys), a square minimap in the top-right corner (since 2026-10-03 it also shows while building, small size only since M is Mirror there - `drawBuildMap`, redrawn from the blocks at most every 1.5s after an edit via `_mapVersion`, bumped in `placeBlock`/`removeBlock`/`clearAllBlocks`; hidden under the pause menu/picker) (`#build-try-map`, a one-pixel-per-column top-down picture of the map from `BuildMode._buildTopDownMap()`, built on entering Try Map, plus a gold arrow for you; **M** cycles normal -> big -> big and see-through in the middle of the screen -> normal) and a center crosshair `#build-try-crosshair` (a + since 2026-10-10, bars drawn by its ::before/::after; the `.hit` flash animates those) (uses the game's `--crosshair-color/size`; since 2026-10-03 it shows the whole time the editor is open, building too - shown in `enter()`, hidden in `exit()`); clicks fire the gun (`audioEngine.playShot`) and nothing is built while trying - except **windows**: the first shot at a glass block (`_isWindowType`: un-shaped `pattern: 'glass'` blocks whose id contains `glass`, so not honey/slime) cracks it (a pixel-art crack overlay, one InstancedMesh), the second breaks it (shards + `playGlassCrack`/`playGlassBreak` in `Audio.js`) and later shots pass through. Broken windows are removed with `_suppressUndoRecording` (no undo entry, no autosave), put back by `exit()`, forgotten by `clearAllBlocks()`, and `_snapshot()` always includes them (`brokenWindows()`), so a save/export/autosave mid-try never stores the holes. - **Map Editor area tools, sky and slot pictures (2026-10-04).** `BuildTools.js` (`buildMode.tools`) holds Fill (F), Replace (R), Shapes (G cycles wall -> floor -> hollow box -> circle -> ball -> off) and Turn Copy (Q, a quarter turn of `_clipboard`, stairs/door facings turned with it - copies now carry `state`); keys live in `FIXED_KEYS` (`buildFill`/`buildReplace`/`buildShape`/`buildRotate`). Each is two right-clicks with a gold preview box (`tools.update()` from `render()`), capped at `MAX_TOOL_CELLS`; Fill/Shapes only fill empty cells, never doors. **Undo is grouped now:** anything that changes many cells runs inside `bm._undoGroupDo(fn)`, recorded as one `{action: 'group', entries}` undo entry (`_applyUndoEntry`) - paste, line, mirrored clicks and every area tool use it; wrap any new multi-cell tool the same way. `#build-tool-hint` (above the hotbar) says which tool is on and what the next click does - Line/Copy use it too. `BuildSky.js` (`buildMode.sky`): Day/Sunset/Night/Cycle from the pause screen, stored in `localStorage['buildmode-sky']`; it only recolors the editor's existing hemisphere + sun light and `scene.background` (still no lights added) and adds stars (Points). **Its weather is Map 1's own (Gaymi preferred it over the editor's first 3D rain/snow, 2026-10-04):** Clear / Light Rain / Hard Rain / Light Snow / Hard Snow / Sandstorm / Random (Map 1's `_rollWeather` odds, re-rolled every few minutes) switch Game.js's `#screen-overlays` (`rainOverlayEl`/`rainOverlayHardEl`/`snowOverlayEl`/`snowOverlayHardEl`/`sandstormOverlayEl`, particles built by `_ensureAllWeatherParticles()`), `audioEngine.setWeatherAudio`, and hard rain's `#lightning-flash` + `playThunder`. They're only shown while the editor is open (`sky.onEnter()`/`onExit()` from `enter()`/`exit()`; `_enterBuildMode` hides the run's own weather overlays first). Old saved `rain`/`snow` prefs map to the light versions. Slot pictures: `_saveSlotThumb()` (on opening the pause screen, before switching slots, on exit) crops the canvas right after a render into a 96x54 JPEG at `localStorage['buildmode-thumb-<slot>']` (device-only, not `gayz-`), shown as the slot button's background (`.has-thumb`). `#build-slots` is a 6-column grid (enter() sets `display: grid` - it used to set flex, which ignored the CSS grid), and `#build-menu-buttons` is a 2-column grid (Try Map/Play/Exit span both); the right column scrolls on short windows.

- **Map Editor game blocks and Play (2026-10-04).** Six `game: true` blocks lead the picker's Special category: `playerstart` (shape `pad`), `zombiespawner` (`cage`, see-through, DoubleSide), `lootchest` (`chest`, latch on its -z front), `lever`, `pressureplate` (`pad`), `sign` (Glowing Sign). They're shaped, so the slab/stairs loops skip them (no "Spawner Stairs"); textures are `OVERRIDE` painters in `BlockTextures.js` (lever/sign use one picture split in halves via `_halfUV`; their picker icons come from `gadgetIconURL`). **Facing is generalized:** every shape in `FACED_SHAPES` (stairs, chest, lever, sign, cage, pad) stores its facing in `_stairFacing`, and its matrix comes from `gadgets.matrixAt`; the place-undo entry is now recorded *after* the facing is set (before, redo of stairs re-aimed them at the camera). `BuildGadgets.js` (`buildMode.gadgets`) keeps lever on/off (`leverOn`, saved as `open` in the door list), sign words (`signText`, saved in a new `s` list in `_encodeSlot` - old saves just have none) with one glowing text Mesh per sign, and pressure plates (`updatePlates`, fed the player's and zombies' feet cells by `BuildTryMode.update`); a lever/plate opens every door within `LINK_RANGE`. `BuildSurvival.js` (`buildMode.survival`) is Play: it runs on top of Try Map (`fire()` asks `tryFire()`/`shoot()` first; `toggleTryMode` leaving Try stops it with `stop({ leaveTry: false })` - don't call `stop()` plain from there or it re-enters `toggleTryMode`), zombies are `buildTexturedCharacter` from a painted skin (only the base-layer areas are filled - filling the overlay areas made solid green shells), and they navigate a flood fill (`_rebuildFlow`, bucket queue from the player's feet, `PATH_MAX_NODES`) over standable cells, bashing closed doors open. `_raycastGridAligned` now also returns `t` (world distance) so a zombie is only hit if it's nearer than the wall. Best wave per slot: `localStorage['buildmode-play-best']`. **Game Mode's Map 3 card now picks Map 3 for the Play button** (`settings.playMap`, `_renderMapSelect`); Play then calls `_enterBuildMode({ map: 'map3', play: true })`. Map 3 got a Player Start (open street cell nearest the middle) and every third crate became a Loot Chest - both as the generator's last steps, so nothing else moved and the edits key wasn't bumped. A map with no Player Start starts you on the open ground nearest the camera (`_defaultStart`).

- **Share codes, Community Maps and Build Together (2026-10-04).** Three new Firestore areas in `FIRESTORE_SECURITY_RULES` (the owner must re-paste the WHOLE ruleset into Firebase Console for them to work - until then every call fails and the UI just toasts "couldn't reach the server"): `sharedMaps/{code}` (doc id = 6-char code from `makeShareCode`, no 0/O/1/I; `base` 'blank' = `_encodeSlot` string, 'map3' = the `_map3Diff()` JSON; `likes` may only go +1 in the same batch that creates `likes/{uid}` - `existsAfter` in the rule; `plays` +1 by anyone), its `likes`/`reports` subcollections, and `buildRooms/{code}` (host's map at room start) + `edits/{id}` (`ops` is a JSON *string* - Firestore can't store arrays of arrays) + `players/{uid}` (eye position + yaw, ~1/s). `BuildShare.js` (`buildMode.share`, the Community Maps panel; Share Map/Community Maps are the old Publish/Browse buttons, wired in Game.js) and `BuildTogether.js` (`buildMode.together`). Anything opened from someone else lands in the **Shared Map slot** (`SHARED_SLOT = 'shared'`, `loadSharedData()`, name in `localStorage['buildmode-shared-info']`) - never in the player's own slots. **Build Together syncs through the undo history:** `_recordUndo` (every non-group entry), `undo()`/`redo()` and `toggleDoor`/`setLever`/`setSignText` call `together.record*`, so any new editing tool that records undo entries syncs for free - flowing liquid doesn't (it runs with undo suppressed, and every client flows its own). Remote ops are applied with `_suppressUndoRecording` + `together.applyingRemote` (not in your undo, not echoed back), and are untrusted: coordinates bounded, block ids checked with `BuildMode.isBlockType`. Old `communityBuilds` docs are still listed (read-only, "Older builds"); `publishBuild`/`reportBuild` are gone. Test seams: `game.__mapShareBackendForTests` / `game.__buildRoomBackendForTests` (see the test in `tests/buildmode.spec.js`).
