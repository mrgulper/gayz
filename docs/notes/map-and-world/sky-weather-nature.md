## 2026-10-26: rainbow, graphics levels upgrade, World Map without zombies
- **Rainbow:** BuildSky `RAINBOW_CHANCE` 0.1 rolled in `update` when a Random step goes from a rain/hail ladder kind to `clear`; `showRainbow()` (daytime only) sets `_rainbowIn` = `RAINBOW_S` 150; `rainbow` eases 0..1 (`RAINBOW_FADE`), ends on new weather or nightfall. SkyBodies draws it on one additive card `RAINBOW_D` 110 away round the anti-sun point (centre clamped 3-24 deg below the horizon so it shows at noon), 42 deg primary + 51 deg secondary computed per pixel from the angle.
- **Graphics levels:** LEVEL_LOOK gained `grade` (finishing colour + soft highlight roll-off above 0.6), `ao` (corner shade, was `0.55*uReal`), `spec` (sun glint added after the sun's shadow in `lights_fragment_end`, using `directLight` = the scene's only DirectionalLight; glossier on `wetNow`/puddles) and `soft` (`sunLight.shadow.radius`; PCFSoftShadowMap is deprecated in r185). Detailed now has shadows (low) + bump + tufts; Balanced has sway + water reflection (`reflect` no longer needs bump). Speed: `vPatch` (big colour patches) per vertex, grain skipped past `grainNear`. Existing players keep their saved shadow/view settings until they pick a level again.
- **World Map mode has no zombies:** `NO_ZOMBIE_MODES` (PlayRules) -> `cfg.noZombies`; BuildSurvival skips waves/spawns, HUD shows "World Map · Time m:ss" ticking each second. Globetrotter = a World Map run of 600 s+ (ProgressUI `end`).
- 5 zombie modes have a real play-through test each in buildmode.spec (`<mode>: starts, spawns zombies, clears waves and ends`).
- **Lumpy, gap-free smooth ground (2026-10-26, Gaymi: "dont make them see thought able ... use more irregular shapes").** BlockChunks `cornerH` pins a corner to 1 when a same-level opaque block that keeps its flat top (road, planks) touches it, and to at least 1 beside a covered block (wall) - those neighbours' sides toward the ground are culled, so a dip or rise left see-through gaps. Smooth tops are drawn by `_lumpyTop` as a 3x3 patch: edges straight between corners (so neighbours/side faces still meet), inner points jittered up/down and sideways by `hash01`, normals tipped by the lumps, AO + block light blended. `_pebble` is a lumpy 6-sided rock with flat facets instead of a box.

## Rounded water, free mouse, camp-safe spiders, real snow/hail (2026-10-26)
- Water tops: BlockChunks sets `aEdge` on water top faces (bits 1/2/4/8 = opaque at -x/+x/-z/+z, always uploaded for the water mesh); the water shader discards outside a radius-0.5 quarter circle where two shore sides meet. Inside (concave) shore corners are still square.
- Wet ground/puddles: `openSky` blends the roof grid over the 4 nearest cells (tent + smoothstep 0.3..0.7), so dry/wet edges are rounded. Ground hail stones are round pellets on a 1/9-block grid.
- Mouse: `BuildMode.mouseScreenOpen()` (pause, inventory, weapon pick, You Died, spawn map, trader, Share, Together, big map). A `pointerlockchange` listener lets go of the mouse if a lock lands while one is open - a requestPointerLock() made just before such a screen (Play Again calls start() then requestPointerLock()) used to land after the screen's exitPointerLock(). `_onPointerDown` won't re-lock while one is open.

- BuildWeather: `uIce` (hail pellets with streak + bouncing splash points via `uBounce`), snow flakes vary in size/shape and flutter in x and z, rain drops vary length/width.
