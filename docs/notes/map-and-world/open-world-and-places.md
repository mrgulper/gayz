Split out of the old `map-and-world.md` on 2026-11-13 (word for word, oldest first). Add new notes at the end.

- **Open world around Map 1 (2026-10-12, Gaymi: "make outside of the map 1 open world with all the biomes on earth", like bloxd.io).** `src/game/OpenWorld.js` (`buildMode.world`), on whenever the block city is loaded (`_applyMap3` calls `world.start(removed)` + `cityLoaded()`; `clearAllBlocks` resets it). Land is never stored: `sampleLand(x, z)` (pure, seeded value-noise fbm: continentalness, temperature, humidity, ridged mountains, mesas, swamps, rivers, blended to y=-1 near the city with `cityDistance`) gives height + one of 26 `BIOMES` (names `biome*` i18n keys); columns (16x16 chunk columns, never the city's own chunks -4..3) are grown around the player / flying camera / server players nearest first within `GEN_BUDGET_MS` per frame (`update(points)` from `BuildMode._updateWorld`, wrapped in try/catch so a bug can't stop the frame loop), straight into `_blocks` via `_setTerrainBlock` (no undo, liquids, lights - water is static until a player edit next to it), and their chunks rebuild through `BlockChunks.markLazy` (`LAZY_BUDGET_MS`, nearest first; edits still rebuild at once). Trees/cacti/ice spikes/mushrooms/coral/bamboo come from a jittered `GRID` and each column only writes the parts landing inside it, so columns match without knowing each other. Far unedited columns unload. **A new world every game (2026-10-12, "regenerate the whole biome again every time a new player creates or joins the map"):** `world.reseed(seed)` (from `Game._enterBuildMode` after `enter()`: random, or `seedFromText(server.serverId)` on a server so everyone gets the same land; `BuildTogether._start` uses the room code) drops all land and anything built outside the city and sets `WORLD_SALT`, which `hash2` mixes into every noise/feature number. So **nothing outside the city is saved**: generated keys (`terrainKeys`) are skipped by `_snapshot`, `_map3Diff` only compares city cells (`cityCell`) and `_applyMap3` drops placed entries outside it; breaking land (`world.touched`) only marks it `removed`/`edited` for the current game. The roads leave through 8 gates (`_findPaths`: border cells with ground and nothing above) and carry on `PATH_LEN` cells as gravel/coarse-dirt tracks. Try Map is clamped to `WORLD_LIMIT` instead of the 128 square, liquids may spread there too, view distance/fog are capped at `world.radius()` (MAX_RADIUS 144), the minimap shows a `MAP_WORLD_SPAN` window centred on you from cached 32x32 noise tiles (`mapTile`) plus the city picture (`_buildTopDownMap` reads only the city's chunks then), and `#build-try-biome` names the biome under it. `BuildSurvival._findBlocks` caches per `_mapVersion` (terrain doesn't bump it). Measured: ~3.4ms per column, ~1.5ms per chunk build (software GL); 130-250k blocks loaded at the default radius. Tests: "Map 1 has an open world with biomes around it" and "every open world biome is within reach of Map 1" (tests/buildmode.spec.js). Changing the noise changes the land for everyone and can misplace saved terrain edits - like the generator, treat it as a map change.

## 2026-10-17 (batch 3): spawn map, endless world, pyramids, underground cities

- **Spawn map** (`SpawnMap.js` + `EarthMapData.js`): a 360x180 1-degree grid of Earth biomes (run-length coded; made by a one-off script from Natural Earth 110m land via world-atlas@2.0.2, public domain, plus hand-written region boxes for deserts/jungles/mountains/snow). `BuildSurvival.start` opens it for fresh solo homepage games when the open world is on (`_chooseSpawn`), before the weapon picker; `_pickWeapon` closes it (camp) if still open, so tests that pick a weapon straight away are unaffected. `findSpawn(lat, lon, biome)` spirals out from `(lon*DEG, -lat*DEG)` for that biome, then `SIMILAR` ones, then any dry land, never within `CITY_KEEP_OUT`. `_spawnAt` grows the column, stands you on its top block and resets `tryMode._spawn`. Servers skip the map.
- **No world edge:** `WORLD_LIMIT = Infinity` (OpenWorld). Very far out (hundreds of thousands of blocks) float precision will eventually jitter.
- **Pyramids:** `pyramid()` in WorldStructures, desert squares with roll < 0.3 (before villages). Hollow, 2-thick stepped shell, passage on a random side.
- **Underground cities:** `UnderCity.js` pure `cityCell(c, x, y, z)` -> undefined / null (air) / block; one per `UC_GRID` square (chance `UC_CHANCE`), floor `FLOOR_MIN..FLOOR_MAX`. Underground treats them like caves (`_grow` marks air open and city blocks solid with their own type; `implicitSolid` and `reveal` ask the city first). Near a city, Underground grows `CITY_RADIUS`/`CITY_ABOVE` chunks with `CITY_BUDGET_MS`.
- **Top bar:** `TopBar.setFps(fps, detail, ms, gpu)`; `chipName()` shortens the WebGL renderer string; the Show FPS setting hides FPS, MS and chip together.
- **Credits:** the 3D Models tab was removed at Gaymi's request (the gun models are CC BY 4.0 - they were warned attribution is required by that license; `public/models/weapons/CREDITS.txt` still lists the makers).
