# Homepage, menu, panels, inventory, skins and other UI

Moved out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-10-16 (word for word) so it is only read when needed. Newest notes for a topic go at the end of its file.

- **Inventory > Character is a Kirka-style skin grid driven by `INVENTORY_SKINS` (Game.js, 2026-09-30).** A new skin is one entry there (`id`, `name`/`nameKey`, `rarity` from `SKIN_RARITIES`, 64x64 Minecraft-format `dataUrl`, `count(game)` - 0 hides it); card pictures are flat front views drawn from the texture (`skinFrontIconURL`), the left preview is its own `MenuAvatar3D` (`_invSkinAvatar3D`). Clicking a card opens Equip/Market/Sell/Inspect (`#inv-skin-menu`, one element shared by Character and Weapons cards - `_openInventorySkinMenu` moves it next to the clicked card's grid and keeps it inside the card; `_invSkinMenuKind` is `'character'`/`'weapon'`). **Inspect** opens `#inspect-dialog` (Kirka-style: title, rarity pill, owned count, big 3D stage) driven by `InspectViewer.js` - drag turns it on both axes, wheel/pinch zooms, no idle spin (removed 2026-10-10); in the Old theme its box is the torn panel paper with dark ink (`html.ui-theme-old #inspect-box`); it gets a fresh canvas every opening and disposes its renderer (`forceContextLoss`) on close, so it never holds a WebGL context while hidden. A weapon is shown via `_weaponDisplayClone` (shares the game's geometry/materials - never dispose them); a character is a fresh `buildTexturedCharacter` that `_closeInspectDialog` disposes. **The Market is player-to-player (since 2026-10-01 - the game itself sells no skins there).** Inventory's card menu is Equip / Market / Sell / Inspect like Kirka: **Market** opens the trade dialog's price picker (`#trade-dialog`, `_openTradeDialog('list')`, 5% fee shown, `_marketFee`) and writes a `marketListings` Firestore doc (`CloudSync.createMarketListing`) after taking the skin out of the seller's own save; **Sell** sells to the game for the fixed `sell` price (half `MARKET_PRICES[rarity]`; GaygarX 5,000 gems). The Market panel (`#market-panel`, opened by `#market-btn`, which always sits right after Store: `_applyNavOrder` and index.html's anti-flash script give the 8 reorderable buttons even `order`s and Market `coinshop*2+1`; `#build-mode-btn` is `order: 99`) shows active listings (`fetchActiveMarketListings` - no orderBy, sorted client-side, since orderBy + the status filter would need a composite index) with a search bar and Items/Rarity dropdowns (`_renderMarketFilters`; a skin's `kind` defaults to `'character'`, a future weapon skin sets `kind: '<weaponId>'`). Buying is a transaction that flips the listing to `sold` (`buyMarketListing`, so two buyers can't both get it); your own listing opens "Take Back" (`cancelMarketListing`). Sellers are paid when they next open Inventory or the Market (`_claimMarketSales` -> `fetchUnclaimedMarketSales` + `claimMarketSale`, a transaction on `claimed`, so a sale pays once even with two devices). Signed-in Cloud Save account required to list/buy (`_marketUid`). Coins/skins stay client-trusted like everything else here; the `marketListings` rules in `FIRESTORE_SECURITY_RULES` only enforce shape and who may flip which status. Test seam: `game.__marketBackendForTests` (same 6 functions, in memory) - and in Playwright pin `_cloudUid` with a getter, since the real auth restore resets it to null a moment after boot. Market skins (`MarketSkins.js`) are still real `INVENTORY_SKINS` entries players can own and trade; there's currently no in-game source of new copies. Ownership is `shopProgress.charSkins` (`{id: count}`, numbers so Cloud Save's additive merge adds up trades across devices); selling/listing the equipped skin re-equips Default. Equipping (`_equipInventorySkin`) works for any owned entry with no per-skin code. Inventory > Weapons is the same card grid, one Default card per weapon (`_renderInventoryWeapons`); pictures are rendered once from each weapon's viewmodel by `_weaponThumbnails()` on a throwaway WebGLRenderer (own canvas, disposed + `forceContextLoss` after), with a RoomEnvironment - most gun materials are metalness 1 and render pure black without an environment. Hands are left out via `userData.isHand` (set in `Viewmodels.js`'s `buildHand`).

Keep any new designer key off the `gayz-` prefix. GayZ Features was ported earlier the same way (straight into `index.html`), so gayz-features.vercel.app isn't needed either.

- **HD skins - 2x/4x pixels (2026-10-11, Gaymi: "add more pixels, 2x or 4x or even more").** A skin may be 64, 128 or 256 pixels wide: the same Minecraft layout with every pixel split 2x2 or 4x4. The game's `loadSkinTexture` returns `width`/`height` in 64-layout units plus `scale` (UVs stay in those units, only the canvas keeps the real pixels), so `buildTexturedCharacter` needs nothing else; anything cropping a skin picture by hand multiplies by `scale` (`_updateMenuAvatarPhoto`, `skinFrontIconURL`); Upload accepts the three sizes. In Design a Skin (`public/skin-designer/index.html`) the atlas is `SIZE` = 64 x `RES` (`setRes`, the Pixels 1x/2x/4x buttons, saved in `skindesigner-res-v1`, default 2x): editor code works in atlas pixels (`SIZE`, `pixelFaceRects`, `pz()` in the 2D view), the generator still paints in 64 units on `genCanvas` (`atlasCtx` is swapped to it for each stage) and `hdDetail` scales it up with Scale2x/EPX restricted to each face (crisp, never blurred - a first blended version came out soft), then adds hair strands and light. Loaded images never lose pixels (`drawImageToAtlas` picks the larger of the setting and the image), undo entries carry their own size. Game.js accepts designer skins up to 1.5 MB of data URL. **8x and 16x (512/1024 wide) were added the same day** (`RES_LEVELS`): the generator just runs Scale2x more times; the 3D preview's grid texture grows with the atlas (`gridScale()`), the 2D view goes down to 1 screen pixel per atlas pixel, undo keeps fewer steps (8 at 16x - 4 MB each), the palette samples every 2nd/4th pixel, Download Big is twice the skin's size. Generated 16x skins are only ~170 KB as PNG, so they sync and save like any other; servers get a 4x/2x/1x copy under `SERVER_SKIN_MAX` (`serverSkin()` in ServerBrowser.js) instead of the default skin. Test: "Design a Skin works at 8x and 16x" / "the game wears a 16x skin" in `tests/skindesigner.spec.js`. **Pixels changes really change the skin (2026-10-11, "I see no difference"):** the Pixels buttons redraw an unedited generated skin at the new size from the same plan (`lastGen` + `atlasSignature()`, `generateThemedSkin(..., fast)`), and Scale2x-upscale anything else; `hdDetail` adds face-edge shadow, top-edge light, hair strands + shine, cloth weave (not on the base head), skin grain and eye catchlights, scaled by `RES`; `scale2x` keeps the face below the hairline square (`crisp`) and only rounds strong edges (soft shading steps rounded into blobs and slanted mouths). Randomize's note comes from `describePlan(lastPlan)`. "No"/"without" words: `parsePrompt` fills `without` (`NEGATORS`/`NEGATABLE`; negators survive `understandWord`), and every `gear*` painter is wrapped by `skippable()`/`leftOut()` so a look's own gear is left off too. Inspect (`InspectViewer` `MIN_ZOOM`/`MAX_ZOOM`) and the designer's OrbitControls zoom have practically no limit.

- **Design a Skin reads everyday words (2026-10-11, Gaymi: "make it think like a very smart AI because some common words it might not even know").** Still fully offline - no AI service, no API key, no cost. In `public/skin-designer/index.html`, `parsePrompt` first runs every word through `understandWords()`: a known word stays; `WORD_MEANINGS` turns everyday words into what the generator draws ("cowgirl" -> `cowboy girl`, "sexy"/"fancy" -> `stylish`, "goggles" -> `glasses`, "santa" -> `casual red white beard beanie`); word endings are tried (plurals, -ed/-ing/-y...); and a near miss becomes the closest known word (`editDistance`, 1 letter, 2 for words of 7+; `PLAIN_WORDS` are never "corrected"), listed in the result as `Read "pirte" as pirate`. 18 looks were added to `THEMES` (stylish, cowboy, chef, firefighter, farmer, scientist, gamer, athlete, suit, prisoner, sailor, miner, clown, superhero, ghost, alien, skeleton, mummy - each with `THEME_GEAR` from the existing gear painters; the last four are in `NON_HUMAN_THEMES`) plus ~40 `COLOR_WORDS`. Rules that bite: never put a part word (`hair`, `skin`) in `PLAIN_WORDS` - colour/style words read the word after them ("red hair"), and never let a meaning put a colour word right before a person word ("goth" -> `shadow black` made "goth girl" a black skin tone via `SKIN_WORDS`). A new word goes in `WORD_MEANINGS` (or the matching table); test: `tests/skindesigner.spec.js` (it serves three.js from `node_modules`, since the designer's import map points at a CDN).
