What's New

Aug 12, 2026Profile is now signed-in only - if you're not logged in, opening it shows a black screen with a typewriter "Login or Register" prompt instead of your stats/bio/highlights. Sign in and everything's back to normal.
Aug 12, 2026Quests nav button now shows a red dot for a new quest (alongside the existing gold "ready to claim" dot), and Friend List shows a gold dot when someone accepts a request you sent them.
Aug 12, 2026Restyled the "Copied" badge (dark background, gold border, tag icon) and fixed a real bug where every body part's texture rendered upside down on real uploaded Minecraft skins - most noticeable on shoes/pants seen from behind, but it affected every part on every angle.
Aug 12, 2026Big batch of fixes and tweaks: your nickname keeps its real capitalization above your character now, Random only previews a name until you confirm it with the pencil, Points shows a clean whole number, Friend List reordered with a new Status picker (Online/Idle/Do Not Disturb/Offline, with "last seen" for offline friends), Credits now lists Owner/Co-Owner/Testers, What's New is wider, page text is no longer draggable/selectable, copying an ID shows a "Copied" badge, Zombie Survival mode got Build Mode's V-key zoom, C4 is now one key (J) instead of two, the Weapons list moved beside your items in the Tab inventory, healing now caps at 2000 (spawn is still 1000), and the hotbar's 1/2/3 numbers are bigger and reordered (3 on top).
Aug 12, 2026Touched up the default character's skin - a small black patch at the hip is now gray, matching the rest of the design.
Aug 12, 2026Fixed a coordinate bug that made the left leg's pants texture come out garbled/misaligned instead of matching the right leg.
Aug 12, 2026The default character is now your actual minecraftskins.com design, pixel-accurate - "G" logo and all. Also fixed skin uploads rendering as a near-black blob for skins that put their design on the overlay layer (a common thing skin editors do) instead of the base layer - both are now read correctly.
Aug 12, 2026You can now upload a real Minecraft skin PNG (Profile panel) - it's read pixel-for-pixel and mapped onto your character properly, instead of us guessing colors from a screenshot. Works with standard 64x64 skin files. Reset to Default is right next to it if you want the old look back.
Aug 11, 2026Default character now matches a real player-made skin - gray body, black hair/vest/pants, white eyes, and a red nose.
Aug 11, 2026Fixed a real bug where the whole page could load unresponsive - nothing clickable anywhere - for anyone who owned certain permanent upgrades (Marksman, Deadeye, Quick Hands, Stockpile). Sorry about that one.
Aug 11, 2026Fixed a bug where opening Settings from the pause menu mid-run, then closing it, could leave the screen frozen with nothing clickable and no way to resume.
Aug 11, 2026Default character's top is now near-black with a white collar patch. The Tab inventory screen no longer has misaligned numbers - they now line up in a clean column. The Weapons list there now only shows the 3 weapons you're actually carrying instead of every weapon in the game, and the Loadouts section (save/load slots, paste-a-code) has been removed.
Aug 11, 2026Currency bar moved back to the top-left corner, and the coin icon is now a single flat detailed 2D circle. Default character recolored to match a dark-teal top and purple pants. Build Mode no longer locks your mouse the instant you enter - it stays free until you click into the viewport, and Escape releases it again. Removed the "(X/10)" count from the Quests panel title.
Aug 11, 2026Coins/Points/Gems bar moved to the top-right corner with more detailed icons (gems now a real faceted diamond shape). Fixed a bug where copying your ID showed no confirmation. Default character redesigned with a white hoodie, dark pants, brown shoes, black hair, and new two-tone eyes. Removed the dot separators from the Rules & Info / What's New / Credits / Discord row.
Aug 11, 2026Knife redesign - sharper blade, held right-handed now, and a real swing animation instead of a gun-style recoil kick. Rain and snow now genuinely fall from the sky with randomized speed and drift instead of repeating in place. Max health is now 1000. The bottom hotbar is gone - your 3 weapons and their ammo (the knife shows an infinity symbol) now sit above your ammo counter. Your default character got more detail (hair, a mouth, shoes). Removed a cosmetic weapon charm that could get in the way of aiming.
Aug 11, 2026Friend List redesign - your own ID and Add a Friend on one side, your friends and their live online/idle/offline/Do Not Disturb status on the other, plus a friend request count and a Delete All button. New Gems currency (earn 1 per 10 nights survived, up to 10 a run) alongside a Coins/Points/Gems bar on the homepage. Build Mode: smaller blocks, more of them, an orange scrollbar, and you can no longer place a block on top of yourself. Your Minecraft-style character now also shows in your Profile.
Aug 11, 2026Rain and snow now fall as small blocky squares (blue for rain, white for snow) instead of streaks and dots.
Aug 11, 2026Settings got a new General tab and Controls now comes before Graphics; the search bar no longer jumps around between tabs. The Knife can now be reskinned like every other weapon. Profile panel cleanup (removed a couple of confusing stat lines, matched button styling) and What's New no longer shows raw diagnostics info.
Aug 11, 2026Friends is now Friend List - add a friend by their player ID instead of nickname. Your 3D character on the homepage now spins on its own.
Aug 10, 2026Many new Build Mode blocks (71 total), richer block textures, and the V key now eases smoothly in and out of its zoomed view instead of snapping.
Jul 30, 2026Global leaderboard, rank badges, friend comparison, a community poll, and a dozen more small homepage upgrades (login streak calendar, achievement hints, sound test buttons, and more).
Jul 30, 2026Cloud Save - sign in with Google to sync your progress across devices.
Jul 29, 2026A full menu redesign, crafting and deployable turrets, the Flamethrower and Rocket Launcher, weapon upgrades and the Mystery Box, Akimbo pistols and Last Stand self-revive, and CoD-style movement (mantling, sliding, killstreaks).
