Progression & Meta

19 Achievements

Spanning combat, survival and story, each with a hint instead of a bare "???" — plus search, category filter, sort, and a Print list export in the Achievements panel.

Rank & Level System

A Rank Roadmap on the Profile panel and a Class Comparison table across your different class picks.

Career Stats & Profile

Lifetime totals, a stats-over-time chart, a pinnable highlight reel, a Best Run breakdown, an anniversary line, today's session stats, and a "Nearly There" panel toward trackable achievements.

Goals

Pick up to 3 personal goals to track/checklist on your Profile.

Upgrades & Legacy Points

Every Map 1 run earns Legacy Points from its kills and waves. Spend them in Upgrades on boosts that last for every run after: more health and armor, more gun damage, faster reloads, starting coins, a cheaper camp Trader and faster healing in the camp - 14 in all.

Quests

Rolling quests that come and go (kill zombies, reach a wave, finish runs), counted from your Map 1 runs and claimed for coins in the Quests panel. Monthly, Yearly and Lifetime quests are coming soon.

World & Exploration

Build Mode / Map Editor

A standalone creative sandbox: hundreds of Minecraft-style blocks (every one with a slab and stairs), water and lava that flow, free-fly flight, save slots plus the ready-made Map 1 city, Try Map to walk it in first person with a gun, Export/Import Map, Mirror mode, a Line tool, and Copy/Paste for stamping down builds.

Map Editor Building Tools

Fill a whole area with one block, Replace one kind of block with another, and build walls, floors, hollow boxes, circles and balls with two clicks - a gold box shows the area first. Turn a copied build before pasting it, and one Undo takes back a whole fill, shape or paste.

Map Editor Time & Weather

Build by day, at sunset or at night under the stars (or let the day and night go round by themselves), with weather - light or hard rain with lightning and thunder, light or hard snow, a sandstorm, or Random to roll one every few minutes - with rain and wind sounds, in Try Map too.

Play Your Map: Zombie Waves

Press Play on the homepage (or Play in the Map Editor, on any map you build) to survive waves of blocky zombies. They find their way to you round walls, up stairs and ladders, and bash closed doors open; every wave has more of them, a little faster and tougher. Your gun needs ammo, Loot Chests refill it and heal you once a wave, and your best wave on each map is kept.

Choose Your Weapon

Every Map 1 run starts by picking the gun you take in - the Knife, Tomahawk, AK-47, M1911, Revolver, Minigun, Shotgun, AWP, GPMG, Flamethrower, Rocket Launcher, Crossbow, Grenade Launcher, MP5-SD or Void Ripper - each a detailed 3D model with its own damage, fire rate, magazine and reload, shown as bars on its card. Automatic guns keep firing while you hold the button, right-click aims down the sights (right-click again to stop) (zoomed in, steadier, tighter spreads), the Inspect Weapon key turns the gun so you can look at it, the Shotgun and Flamethrower fire spreads, the Rocket and Grenade Launchers blast everything near where they land, and the Knife never runs out. Ammo from chests and the Trader comes in your gun's own amounts, and your last pick is remembered.

Map 1: The Block City & Safe Camp

Map 1 is a ready-made block city with winding roads, parks and buildings you can enter and climb. Playing it starts you in its walled camp, a safe zone with one main gate that zombies can't get into. Four NPCs made in Design a Skin live there: the Trader sells Med Kits, Armor and Ammo Boxes, the Upgrader makes your gun hit harder, reload faster and hold more, Ammo Refill fills you up for free every so often, and the Quest Board gives quests for coins. Talk to one to open its board; close it with the red X or a click outside it. You earn coins from kills and cleared waves, and standing in the camp slowly heals you. The NPCs also stand in the camp while you build on Map 1 in the Map Editor.

Map 1 Game Modes, Difficulty & Mutators

Before you press Play, pick in Game Mode: a difficulty (Easy, Normal, Hard - Nightmare and Apex unlock later), a class (Balanced; Runner, faster but with less health; or Tank, slower but tougher), a game mode - Classic (waves forever), Zombie Defense (hold out for 10 waves to win), Boss Hunt (a big, tough boss every 3 waves), Zombie Rush (barely a breather between waves, a survival timer, and no buying) or Zombie Extraction (after 5 waves a helicopter lands somewhere in the city - follow its red beam and hold its landing ring for 10 seconds while the zombies keep coming) - and mutators: Horde Rush, Loot Rush, Health Regen, Glass House, Iron Mode, Escalation and Boss Rush. Guest Mode plays a run without saving anything from it.

Map Editor Game Blocks

Player Start (where Play puts you), Zombie Spawner (where zombies come from), Loot Chest, Lever (opens the doors near it), Pressure Plate (opens them while someone stands on it) and the Glowing Sign you write your own words on.

Share Codes & Community Maps

Share Map puts your map online under a short code. Anyone can type a code - or pick a map from Community Maps, sorted by Newest, Most Liked or Most Played - and Play it against zombie waves or open it to Edit, in its own Shared Map slot so none of your maps are touched. Like the ones you enjoy (once each), report anything bad, and delete your own.

Build Together

Start a room from your map and send friends its code - everyone in it builds on the same map live, sees each other as blocky figures with name tags, and every block, fill, paste, undo, door, lever and sign shows up for all of them.

Map Editor Slot Pictures

Every save slot shows a little picture of its map on the pause screen, so you can find the one you want.

Customization

Old & Golden Themes

Two complete looks for the menus, picked in Inventory > Theme: Old (the default - rusty metal, torn paper panels, worn profile card) and Golden (gold plates and frames). Both have rain falling over the homepage street, with splashes on the cobbles.

Design a Skin

A built-in skin editor (Design a Skin, at the bottom of the homepage): paint a 64x64 Minecraft-style skin on a live 3D preview, save it to slots, and wear it in game.

Inventory Skins: Rarities, Inspect & Sell

Every character skin card shows its rarity and how many you own - search them by name or pick a rarity. Click one to Equip it, put it on the Market, Sell it to the game for coins, or Inspect it in a big 3D view you can turn and zoom.

Custom Skin Upload

Upload a real Minecraft-format 64x64 skin PNG, read pixel-for-pixel and mapped onto your character, with a Reset to Default option.

Gems Shop Skin

GaygarX - a purchasable character skin in the Shop, priced in Gems (earned from waves survived and kills) - buying it equips it immediately, and it's yours to switch back to any time afterward from the Inventory panel's Character tab, alongside Default.

Nickname Customization

Editable nickname, a color picker, a font picker, a homepage Motto line, and a Bio with preset saving.

Player Title Color & Avatar Frame Tiers

A custom accent color for your stat-line title, plus automatic border-color escalation on your avatar as your level tier climbs.

Homepage Personalization

Accent color, Play-button color, UI font, layout density, background mood, homepage greeting text, and a reorderable nav-button order.

Menu Presets & Sharing

Save Setup preset chips, a Surprise Me randomizer, and a Share panel for your setup, profile, loadout, a friend challenge, or the page link.

Social & Cloud

Servers

The Global panel lists open servers beside the chat, like Kirka: search them, see how many are playing and which wave they're on, join one or create your own, and play Map 1 together - up to eight players see each other, fight the same zombies and waves, and every kill counts for whoever made it. Parkour and Custom servers are coming soon.

Cloud Save

Google Sign-In syncs your save across devices, with a conflict resolver if local and cloud saves diverge.

Leaderboards

Global (with a region filter), Weekly Challenge, Most Achievements, Nearby Rank, a Global Rank badge, a Nearest-Rival nudge, global average/percentile comparison, and a live-subscribed top board.

Global Kill Counter

A live, server-wide total-zombies-killed count that ticks up with everyone's runs.

Friend List

The Friend List button: add by player ID or by name (a list of matching players pops up as you type), real-time friend requests, as many friends as you like with each one's #ID, live Friend Compare, and an online/idle/DND/offline status dropdown with last-seen time.

Market

A player-to-player skin market: list a skin from your Inventory at your own price (5% fee), browse and search everyone's listings by item and rarity, buy one, or take your own listing back. You're paid when you next open the Market or Inventory. Needs a Cloud Save sign-in.

Chat

An always-visible chat box with Global and Clan tabs. Right-click any name to block them - their messages show as "Blocked message - Show" in every channel until you unblock them in Settings. No word filtering otherwise, but spamming gets you muted for 5 minutes.

Login Streak

Counts the days in a row you play: the first visit each day shows a message with your streak and bonus coins, a banked Streak Freeze saves your streak if you miss one day, and coming back after longer gets a welcome-back bonus.

Anonymous Leaderboard Mode

Hide your name on public leaderboards while still competing.

Player ID / Tag System

A stable, copyable ID separate from your editable nickname, used for friend search and leaderboard identity.

Export/Import Save & Settings Sharing

Export/Import Save to a file with a Compare Save diff tool, Copy Save to Clipboard, and separate Settings/Keybind codes to transfer just preferences.

Community Poll & Bug Report Tool

One developer-authored poll with one-vote-per-account, plus a Copy Bug Report button for sharing diagnostics.

Accessibility & Settings

Auto Quality & FPS Cap

Auto Quality (on by default) makes the picture a little less sharp on its own when the game slows down and sharpens it again when it can; an FPS Cap setting limits the frame rate. Both are in Settings > Graphics.

View Distance

Settings > Graphics > View Distance (Auto, Short, Medium, Far, Max) sets how far away the city is drawn - fog fades out everything past it, so a shorter one runs much smoother on phones and older computers. Auto starts shorter on a phone, and if the game is still slow at its lowest resolution it draws less on its own.

Lite Textures & Graphics Recovery

Lite Textures (Settings > Graphics) draws the game's big pictures (like the gun models) at half size, so they use far less graphics memory. If your browser drops the game's graphics connection (often from several 3D games open at once), the game pauses with a small note instead of a big error, picks up right where you were when it comes back, and turns on Lite Textures so it's less likely to happen again.

Visual Accessibility

Colorblind Mode with separate Red-Green (Protanopia/Deuteranopia) and Blue-Yellow (Tritanopia) options and a live preview, Large Text Mode, Dyslexia-Friendly Font, High Contrast Mode, Text Spacing, Button Size, Reduce Transparency, High-Visibility Cursor, caption-style text backgrounds, visible focus rings, underlined links.

Motion & Flash Sensitivity

Reduce Flashing Effects with a live preview, Streaming-Safe Mode, and toast duration control.

Audio Accessibility

Audio cue on hover/focus, Positional Audio, Mute on Tab Blur, a Master Volume slider plus separate SFX (with a Test button) and Ambient sliders.

Input Customization

Rebindable keys for everything you do in Map 1 - moving, jumping, sprinting, crouching, reloading, using doors and people, inspecting your gun and the minimap size - with Export/Import codes, a one-handed layout (mouse side buttons use and reload), and mouse sensitivity, invert look and field of view for the Map Editor and Map 1.

How to Play

A replayable How to Play reference covering moving, shooting, the camp and its NPCs, and the zombie waves.

Rules & Info

Simple conduct rules at the end of How to Play, and basic facts about the game itself in Credits.

Terms of Use & Privacy Policy

Plain-language Terms of Use and Privacy Policy pages, linked side by side at the bottom of the homepage and from Credits.

Language

A language picker covering 12 languages - English, Mandarin Chinese, Hindi, and Spanish are fully supported, with French, Russian, Indonesian, German, Japanese, Vietnamese, Brazilian Portuguese, and Italian marked "Coming soon" - plus a quick-switch homepage button and a "request a missing language" Discord link.

Quality of Life

On a phone or tablet, Map 1 (and Try Map) gets simple touch controls with icons instead of words: a stick that appears under your left thumb (push it all the way to run), drag anywhere else to look, and round buttons for Fire (hold to keep firing), Jump, Reload, Use (doors, chests and the camp's people), Crouch and Pause.

Notification Toggles

Achievement, rank-up, and leaderboard alerts, a weekly challenge reminder, low-coins reminder, friend online/offline alerts.

Save & Account QoL

Auto-save frequency, save-size readout, Clear Cache, storage-full warning, backup reminder, confirm-before-sign-out, telemetry opt-out.

What's New Digest

A structured, dated in-game changelog with an optional "show every launch" toggle, and a small What's New card in the corner of the homepage when GayZ has been updated - close it with the X or let it fade away.

Easter Eggs

A classic Konami code, a hidden zombie icon, a logo click counter, joke tips, and a once-a-year April Fools flip.
