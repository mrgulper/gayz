Special Ammo Guide

Coming Soon - not purchasable yet, this just explains what each one will do.

Incendiary Rounds Sets zombies on fire, dealing burn damage over time.
Ricochet Rounds Bullets bounce to a nearby zombie after the hit, spreading some damage to a second target.
Armor-Piercing Rounds Ignores a zombie's shield/armor instead of getting blocked by it.
Precision Rounds 25% chance per hit to land a critical hit for extra damage.
Electric Rounds Chain-stuns the nearest other zombie near your target (not the one you hit).
Acid Rounds Corrodes the zombie, making it take more damage for a while after being hit.
Cryo Rounds Fully freezes the zombie in place for a few seconds.
