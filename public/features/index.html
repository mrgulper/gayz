Core Gameplay

Combat & Shooting

First-person shooting with recoil, per-weapon camera shake, headshots and critical hits, damage falloff on shotguns, hit markers, and floating damage numbers.

▸15 Firearms + Knife

AK-47, M1911, Minigun (overheats and jams if held too long), Weatie pump shotgun, AWP sniper rifle with scope, Glock 18, Flamethrower, Rocket Launcher (splash damage), Crossbow (retrievable bolts), Grenade Launcher, Suppressed SMG, Nail Gun (pins zombies in place), Harpoon Gun (yanks enemies toward you), and the rare Void Ripper (a slow vortex orb that pulls zombies in before detonating).

AK-47 — reliable all-rounder assault rifle.
M1911 — starter sidearm pistol.
Minigun — huge sustained fire, but overheats and jams if you hold the trigger too long.
Weatie — pump shotgun, damage falls off at range.
AWP — sniper rifle, comes with a built-in scope.
Glock 18 — fast-firing sidearm.
Flamethrower — continuous close-range burn damage.
Rocket Launcher — explosive splash damage.
Crossbow — bolts can be retrieved off zombies after a hit.
Grenade Launcher — lobs explosive rounds.
Suppressed SMG — quiet, doesn't draw as much zombie attention.
Nail Gun — pins zombies in place on hit.
Harpoon Gun — yanks enemies toward you on hit.
Void Ripper — rare drop; fires a slow vortex orb that pulls zombies in before detonating.
Knife — default melee, always available.

▸8 Melee Weapon Variants

Bat, Machete, UV Baton, Fire Axe (cleaves nearby enemies), Sledgehammer (stuns on hit), Spear, Nunchaku — found as loot and swapped in for the knife.

Bat — fast, basic swing.
Machete — sharper, more damage per hit.
UV Baton — glowing melee weapon.
Fire Axe — cleaves multiple nearby enemies in one swing.
Sledgehammer — slow, but stuns on hit.
Spear — longer reach.
Nunchaku — fast multi-hit combo weapon.

Weapon Mastery

Every kill with a gun counts toward that gun's own permanent tally: Mastery at 75 kills (+damage), Grandmaster at 250 (bigger bonus), Legendary at 600 (faster reload). Grandmastering a weapon unlocks its Heirloom cosmetic skin tier.

Akimbo

Dual-wielding, unlockable separately for Pistols and for Shotguns.

Auto-Weapons

Vampire-Survivors-style orbiting/summoned weapons earned via free level-up picks mid-run: Spinning Blades, Homing Drone shots, a Damage Aura, and Chain Lightning — each with its own unlock, upgrade, and evolve path.

▸30 Zombie Types

Feral Pack, Feral Child, Shambler, Runner, Brute, Spitter, Crawler, Burrower (ambushes from below), Bloater (exploder), Riot Corpse (shielded, melee only), Screamer (wakes and enrages nearby zombies), Bomber Spitter, Swarmer, Sewer Dweller, Fester (toxic gas on death), Leaper, Regenerator (self-heals unless burning), Stalker (invisible until close), Seeper (acid trail), Anchor (pulls you with its spit), Brittle (shatters into shrapnel), Siren (disorients your aim), Bloodhound (ignores stealth), Webber (slows with web patches), Vampire (heals off hits landed), plus 4 bosses: Colossus, Broodmother, and the rare roaming Titan.

Feral Pack — fast-moving dogs that attack in groups.
Feral Child — small, fast, erratic movement.
Shambler — the basic slow zombie.
Runner — sprints straight at you.
Brute — tanky, hits hard.
Spitter — ranged acid/projectile attack.
Crawler — low profile, harder to hit center-mass.
Burrower — tunnels underground and ambushes from below.
Bloater — explodes on death or contact.
Riot Corpse — carries a shield; only melee gets through the front.
Screamer — wakes and enrages every zombie nearby.
Bomber Spitter — ranged explosive spit attack.
Swarmer — spawns smaller Shamblers when it dies.
Sewer Dweller — found in the sewer tunnels.
Fester — leaves a toxic gas cloud behind when it dies.
Leaper — jumps at you from a distance.
Regenerator — heals itself back up unless it's on fire.
Stalker — invisible until it's close to you.
Seeper — leaves a damaging acid trail as it moves.
Anchor — its spit attack pulls you toward it.
Brittle — shatters into damaging shrapnel on a melee kill.
Siren — its scream disorients your aim.
Bloodhound — tracks you even through stealth perks.
Webber — slows you down with sticky web patches.
Vampire — heals itself off every hit it lands on you.
Colossus (boss) — huge, heavy-hitting boss zombie.
Broodmother (boss) — spawns Sewer Dwellers during the fight.
Titan (boss) — rare roaming reptilian boss, distinct from scheduled night bosses.

Bestiary

Tracks every zombie type you've personally encountered, unlocks lore blurbs, and feeds the Bestiary Master achievement.

Day/Night Cycle

A real-time 12-minute day and 12-minute night cycle with real lighting and fog transitions.

5 Difficulty Tiers

Easy, Normal, Hard, and two unlockable tiers: Nightmare (unlocked by reaching the true ending) and Apex (unlocked by surviving 15 nights on Nightmare).

▸Random Night Events

One fires per night: Horde Surge, Blackout, Supply Drop, Survivor Found, Camp Attack, Supply Convoy, Toxic Gas, Spreading Toxic Gas, Escort Convoy, Radio Distress Call, EMP Field.

Horde Surge — a sudden wave of extra zombies.
Blackout — the lights go out for the night.
Supply Drop — a crate of supplies lands somewhere on the map.
Survivor Found — an NPC survivor appears to be rescued.
Camp Attack — a small NPC group under active zombie siege you can save.
Supply Convoy — a guarded chest escorted by rival humans.
Toxic Gas — a damaging gas cloud spreads over an area.
Spreading Toxic Gas — a version that keeps expanding over time.
Escort Convoy — protect a moving convoy.
Radio Distress Call — a call leads you to a location needing help.
EMP Field — an area that disables electronics.

Nightly Zombie Mutations

Random modifiers that can apply to the whole horde on a given night: Armored, Swift, Reckless, or Docile.

Killstreak Rewards

Consecutive kills grant tiered bonuses: a damage boost at 5, a screen-clearing airstrike at 10, temporary infinite ammo at 15.

Last Stand

A once-per-run self-revive: instead of dying, you're knocked down at 1 HP with a pistol on a timer — enough kills while downed revives you.

Mystery Box

Spend points at a physical machine for a random weapon reroll, with a smaller chance at a rare one.

Barricaded Windows

Fixed chokepoints where zombies tear planks off over time — repairing them earns points, with siege escalation and horde-size multipliers on how fast they get torn down.

Chests & Loot

Lootable crates with a weighted loot table (health, ammo, melee weapons, rare/legendary parts, grenades, rations), plus a guaranteed-good-reward Vault room.

Turret Kit & Med Station

A deployable turret that auto-tracks and fires at the nearest zombie (3 upgrade tiers), and a deployable station that heals you and nearby companions over time.

Vehicles

Drivable Car and Motorcycle with distinct speed/fuel/durability tradeoffs, crash damage, a separate Nitro Boost resource, a toggleable radio, and Trader-purchasable armor/repairs.

▸Inventory Items

Health Pack, Armor Pack, Noisemaker, Grenade, Barricade, Spike Trap, Molotov, C4, Adrenaline Shot, EMP Grenade, Riot Shield, Throwing Knife, Turret Kit, Med Station Kit, Alarm Kit, Ration, Water Bottle — each on its own hotkey.

Health Pack — restores health.
Armor Pack — restores armor.
Noisemaker — thrown, distracts zombies toward it.
Grenade — thrown explosive.
Barricade — placeable blockade.
Spike Trap — placeable, damages zombies that walk over it.
Molotov — thrown fire damage over an area.
C4 — plantable remote-detonated explosive.
Adrenaline Shot — temporary combat boost.
EMP Grenade — disables electronics in an area.
Riot Shield — blocks damage from the front.
Throwing Knife — thrown melee weapon.
Turret Kit — deploys an auto-firing turret.
Med Station Kit — deploys a healing station.
Alarm Kit — deployable alarm.
Ration — restores hunger.
Water Bottle — restores thirst.

Movement

Sprint, crouch, jump, mantling over obstacles, sliding, and a dodge-dash.

Status Systems

Health, Armor, Stamina, Hunger, Thirst, Warmth, and flashlight Battery, each with its own HUD bar, plus an Infection indicator.

Weapon Wheel

A radial hold-to-select weapon switcher, separate from number-key switching.

Photo Mode & Spectate Mode

A free-fly camera with selectable filters for screenshots, and a separate free-fly Spectate option from the pause menu to watch without playing.

Screenshot & Recording Tools

In-game screenshot capture with crop selection and captions, plus a Start/Stop clip recorder that auto-saves on stop, death, quit, or a won run.

Death Screen

Survival stats, a letter death grade, run highlights, cause-of-death breakdown, a small replay, Legacy Points earned, and a Share Run Card / Copy Text Recap option.

Story Mode

8 collectible Audio Logs telling the VIREO conspiracy plot, a VIREO Terminal to interact with, and a true "Ground Truth" ending.

Game Modes & Mutators

▸Game Modes

Classic (base survive-the-nights loop), Zombie Defense (protect a base through 10 waves), Boss Hunt (track down and defeat a random boss), Zombie Rush (endless escalating swarm).

Classic — the base survive-the-nights loop.
Zombie Defense — protect a base through 10 waves.
Boss Hunt — track down and defeat a randomly-picked boss.
Zombie Rush — endless escalating swarm, survive as long as you can.

▸21 Mutators

Horde Rush (2x zombies), Loot Rush (2x XP & points), Pure Gunplay (no auto-weapons), Boss Rush (a boss every night), Horde Mode (relentless close-range waves), Health Regen, Iron Mode (no Trader/Shop spending), Scavenger Run (start with just melee), Glass House (2x damage dealt and taken), Featured Enemy (one zombie type spawns far more), Blackout (near-zero light), Boss Gauntlet (bosses spawn back-to-back), King of the Hill, Extraction, Zombie Defense & Zombie Rush toggle variants, Bounty Hunt, Escalation Mode (difficulty never caps), Randomizer Mode (random weapon each run), Cursed Run (that week's 3 rotating cursed mutators forced on together), Daily Challenge, and the Weekly Featured Mutator with a Weekly Remix combo bonus.

Horde Rush — 2x zombies.
Loot Rush — 2x XP and points.
Pure Gunplay — no auto-weapons allowed.
Boss Rush — a boss spawns every night.
Horde Mode — relentless close-range waves.
Health Regen — slowly self-heal after a few seconds out of combat.
Iron Mode — no Trader or Coin Shop spending allowed.
Scavenger Run — start with just melee, earn the rest.
Glass House — 2x damage dealt and taken.
Featured Enemy — one random zombie type spawns far more often.
Blackout — near-zero ambient light.
Boss Gauntlet — the next boss spawns immediately after the last dies.
King of the Hill — hold a zone for bonus points.
Extraction — reach and hold a landing zone to win the run.
Zombie Defense / Zombie Rush toggles — mutator-style versions of those game modes.
Bounty Hunt — track down and defeat a boss.
Escalation Mode — difficulty keeps climbing with no cap.
Randomizer Mode — your weapon pick each run is randomized.
Cursed Run — forces on that week's 3 rotating cursed mutators together.
Daily Challenge — a fixed daily twist with its own leaderboard.
Weekly Featured Mutator — one rotating mutator highlighted each week, with a coin bonus and a Weekly Remix combo bonus.

Other Run Toggles

Hardcore Mode (no respawn), Guest Mode (don't save the run), Endless Mode, Score Attack Mode.

Weekly Challenges

3 rotating challenges (Headhunter, Exterminator, Sharpshooter) — the same ones for everyone each real-world week, with a coin reward and their own leaderboard.

Bounty Board

One active side-objective at a time from the Trader (kill counts, survive a weather night, reach a night, rescue survivors, clear a location) — rewards points and rolls a fresh one on completion.

Setup/Challenge Codes

Export a shareable code encoding your exact mutator setup so a friend can paste it in and match it instantly.

Progression & Meta

Perks

Offered 3-at-a-time from points earned in a run: Fast Reload, More Stamina, Better Armor, Stronger Heals, Sprint Boost, Dead Silence, Insurance, Barricade Medic, Pickup Magnet — rerollable or skippable.

Perk Synergies

3 bonus effects that unlock once you've ever owned both perks of a specific pair, rewarding committing to a build: Combat Reflexes, Iron Resolve, Fortified.

XP Level-Up Picks

Free passive buffs on every level-up: Move Speed, Max Health, Damage, Stamina, Armor, plus two once-only tradeoff picks — Glass Cannon and Berserker.

Permanent Upgrades

~39 one-time purchases from Legacy Points, across Survival/Combat/Utility trees plus base-building unlocks (turret tiers, walls, watchtower, farm plot), companion perks, night vision, and lifetime-coin-gated Veteran's Caches.

Prestige

Reset your Legacy Points for a permanent Prestige tier, with a full history log of every past prestige and color-tier badges on your homepage and Profile.

Respec

Refund and re-spend all Permanent Upgrade points.

21 Achievements

Spanning combat, survival, exploration, story, and collection, each with a hint instead of a bare "???" — plus search, category filter, sort, and a Print list export in the Achievements panel.

Weapon Evolution

Fully upgrading and evolving an auto-weapon (Blades or Homing) counts toward its own achievement.

Lifetime Quests

22 permanent one-time milestones across career kills, best-ever killstreak, and lifetime points — each pays out coins once, ever.

Rolling Quests

Every player sees the same 5 active quests at the same real-world moment, refreshing every 30 minutes, no backend required.

Rank & Level System

An XP-level HUD badge, a Rank Roadmap on the Profile panel, and a Class Comparison table across your different class picks.

Career Stats & Profile

Lifetime totals, a stats-over-time chart, a pinnable highlight reel, a Best Run breakdown, an anniversary line, today's session stats, and a "Nearly There" panel toward trackable achievements.

Goals

Pick up to 3 personal goals to track/checklist on your Profile.

World & Exploration

Named Locations

Park, Hospital, Pharmacy, Gun Shop, Police Station, Military Checkpoint, Prison, University, Office Skyscrapers with rooftop shortcuts, Mega Mall, Warehouse, Gas Station, Bank with its own vault, Diner, Radio Station, Fire Station, Motel, and dozens of smaller storefronts.

46 Walkable Skyscrapers

Every tall building that used to be pure decoration is now a real, enterable skyscraper with floors and stairs, automatically spaced so none of them touch or overlap.

Full Subway Network

Junction rooms, connector tunnels, two entrances, a maintenance tunnel network, a wrecked train chamber, a full underground station, a toxic sewer level, and a mine level — a genuinely explorable underground biome.

Sewer System & Vireo Facility

A separate tunnel network home to the Sewer Dweller and Broodmother boss, and a story-specific hidden location tied to the audio-log narrative.

Safe Zone / Trader Compound

A guarded hub with passive regen, home to the Trader (Salvage, Crafting, Stash, Sellback, rotating Black Market), a Practice Range, Trophy Wall, the Vault, Ammo Station, Weapon Upgrade Machine, and the Mystery Box.

Explorable Interiors

A Hidden Complex accessible from certain doors, an Elevator Tower, Fire Escapes, and rooftop shortcuts between skyscrapers.

Airdrops

Supply crates you can race a squad of Rival Scavengers to — whoever gets there first claims it, or the rivals fight if you close the distance first.

Compass, Full Map & Minimap

HUD markers for Trader, Ammo, Vehicle, Airdrop, Vault, and Subway, plus a full map screen and an adjustable-zoom minimap.

Build Mode / Map Editor

A standalone creative sandbox: 71+ placeable blocks, free-fly flight, 3 save slots, Export/Import Map, Mirror mode, a Line tool, and Copy/Paste for stamping down builds.

Environmental Effects

Rain, snow, sandstorms, lightning, a night-vision overlay, and real rain/snow ambience sound that plays and fades correctly on every session.

Interactive Props

A Jukebox, Bulletin Board, Hall of Fame, Payphone, Jump Pad, Scaffolding, Wrecking Pendulum, Breaker Box, wandering Pet, Generator, Explosive Barrels, and a Manhole Cover.

Customization

Classes / Loadouts

Pick a Companion role (Ranged, Melee-Bruiser, Medic) merged with a player build (Balanced, Runner, Tank) in one Choose Class grid.

Companion NPC

A controllable AI ally with 3 roles, a custom name and colors, gear, a dedicated perk tree, a Speed Boost upgrade, and an Auto-Revive upgrade — plus Follow/Hold Position/Focus Fire squad orders.

Cosmetic Outfits & Hats

7 outfits and 3 hats purchasable with coins, visible in third person.

Custom Skin Upload

Upload a real Minecraft-format 64x64 skin PNG, read pixel-for-pixel and mapped onto your character, with a Reset to Default option.

Nickname Customization

Editable nickname, a color picker, a font picker, a homepage Motto line, and a Bio with preset saving.

Player Title Color & Avatar Frame Tiers

A custom accent color for your stat-line title, plus automatic border-color escalation on your avatar as your level tier climbs.

Homepage Personalization

Accent color, Play-button color, UI font, layout density, background mood, homepage greeting text, and a reorderable nav-button order.

Menu Presets & Sharing

Save Setup preset chips, a Surprise Me randomizer, and a Share panel for your setup, profile, loadout, a friend challenge, or the page link.

Social & Cloud

Leaderboards

Global (with a region filter), Weekly Challenge, Most Achievements, Nearby Rank, a Global Rank badge, a Nearest-Rival nudge, global average/percentile comparison, a live-subscribed top board, plus Boss Rush, Hardcore Memorial, Household, and Daily Challenge boards.

Global Kill Counter

Friend List

Real-Time Multiplayer

Login-Streak Calendar

Export/Import Save & Settings Sharing

Community Poll & Bug Report Tool

Language

Downtown Core

Underground (not shown above)

  • Subway — tunnel network below downtown, reached via the station entrance.
  • Sewer — separate tunnel branch, its own entrance near downtown.
  • Vireo Facility — a continuation of the subway tunnel, not a separate entrance.
  • Toxic Sewer Level — a deeper, hazard variant branch.

Far East

Past the main map: a standalone Office Skyscraper at x = 250 with its own rooftop bunker, and the Mega Mall further out at x = 340, connected by a diagonal walkway.

Coordinates pulled directly from World.js's buildWorld() — positions are exact, distances between clusters are roughly to scale, individual building footprints are not.
