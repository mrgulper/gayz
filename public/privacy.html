Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 - this is a small hobby project maintained by one person, so this page may change as the game does. Check back if you want the latest version.

What GayZ is

GayZ is a free browser-based zombie survival game. There's no company behind it - it's a personal project. This page explains what data the game collects and why, in plain language.

If you don't sign in

By default, GayZ doesn't need an account. Your progress, settings, and stats are saved only in your own browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nobody but you can see it.

If you sign in with Google (Cloud Save)

  • Signing in uses Google's own sign-in (Firebase Authentication) - GayZ never sees or stores your Google password.
  • Your basic account info (name, email, profile photo) is used only to identify your save data, not for ads or marketing.
  • Your save data (progress, coins, achievements, settings) is stored in Firebase (a Google-hosted database) so it can follow you across devices.

Nicknames, profiles, and leaderboards

Anything you type into a nickname, bio, or profile field is visible to other players if you use public features like leaderboards or friend comparisons. Please don't put personal information (real name, contact details, etc.) in fields other players can see.

Anonymous usage counters

A few features track simple aggregate counts (like how often a setting gets opened) to understand what's actually used. These are just totals - they aren't tied to your account or identity in any readable way.

What GayZ does not do

  • No ads, no ad tracking, no selling data to anyone.
  • No real-money payments - all in-game currency (coins/points) is just for the game, not real money.
  • No tracking you across other websites.

Your choices

You can play entirely without signing in. If you have signed in and want your cloud save or account data deleted, or have any other question about your data, reach out on Discord (link below) and it'll be handled directly.
